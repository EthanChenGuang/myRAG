# The myRAG name and logo

The code is MIT-licensed. The name "myRAG" and the myRAG logo are not part
of that license.

**Fine without asking:**

- Saying your product is "built with myRAG" or "based on myRAG".
- Running myRAG, modified or not, for yourself, your company, or your
  clients, including with the default branding left in place.
- Writing about myRAG, teaching it, or linking to it.

**Needs a different name:**

- Publishing, distributing, or selling a fork, template, starter kit, or hosted
  service. Call it something that isn't "myRAG" and doesn't look like it, and
  swap out the logo in `public/`.
- Anything that suggests your version is the official one or is endorsed by
  the myRAG project.

If you're unsure, ask at hello@myrag.ai.
