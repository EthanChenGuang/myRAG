## Verdict

**The `duplicate_flood` loss comes from BM25 itself: IDF on a corpus of duplicates. It is not caused by score ties, and de-duplication does not fix it.**

`templated_collection` does not drop beyond the noise band on this branch, so there is nothing to attribute there. Its Recall@10 moves by one span on 58 items.

## Evidence

### 1. Which span is lost

Every `duplicate_flood` question has two answer spans:

- the "flood" passage, copied into 30 documents (`dup-passage-01..30`);
- one distinct passage in `dup-solo`.

In both arms, the `dup-solo` chunk is at rank 1 of the kept list for every item (MRR 1.0). What bm25 loses is the flood passage.

- **With `ts_rank_cd`:** copies of the flood passage fill ranks 2 to 8 (Italian items) or 5 to 8 (English items).
- **With bm25:** the copies drop out of the top 5 more often. Over all 9 items (gated split included):
  - prechunked: 4 items miss the flood span at rank 5 under bm25, against 1 under `ts_rank_cd`;
  - ingest: 4 items miss it under bm25, against 3 under `ts_rank_cd`. Their places go to unrelated fixture chunks that match only the question's common words, for example refund, warranty and security-fixture sections.

### 2. Why: IDF

The query terms that pick out the flood passage occur in 30 documents of a small fixture tenant.

- **BM25:** the IDF of those terms is low (Lucene IDF with df = 30), so the copies score below chunks that match rarer, generic terms.
- **`ts_rank_cd`:** it has no IDF, so the copies keep their high lexical score.

This is BM25 working as designed: a term spread over many documents carries little information. On this corpus, though, the spread comes from duplicate documents, not from vocabulary.

### 3. Not ties

| Mode | Arm | Items with a tie in the lexical top 10 | Items with a tie across rank 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| prechunked | bm25 | 9 of 9 | 9 of 9 |
| prechunked | `ts_rank_cd` | 9 of 9 | 9 of 9 |
| ingest | bm25 | 9 of 9 | 9 of 9 |
| ingest | `ts_rank_cd` | 9 of 9 | 9 of 9 |

- The counts are the same in both arms. 9 items includes the non-gated split.
- On average, `ts_rank_cd` ties slightly more top-10 hits than bm25: 9.4 against 8.9 in prechunked.
- Identical copies get identical scores under either scorer, so ties are not what separates the arms.
- The "duplicate tie" column of `dup-diagnostic.md` is strict: every member of a tie group must be a near duplicate of every other. Mixed groups therefore count as 0.

### 4. De-duplication does not help

| Mode | Group | bm25 R@5 | `ts_rank_cd` R@5 | bm25 R@10 | `ts_rank_cd` R@10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| prechunked | baseline | 0.7500 | 1.0000 | 0.9167 | 1.0000 |
| prechunked | `RAG_DUP_CLUSTERS=true` | 0.7500 | 1.0000 | 0.9167 | 1.0000 |
| prechunked | `+ RAG_RESULT_DIVERSITY=collapse` | 0.7500 | 1.0000 | 0.9167 | 1.0000 |

- In prechunked mode the harness builds the clusters (`dup_clusters:eval_run` in the run manifest). Collapse acts on the traces: `also_in` appears on 36 traces.
- Collapse folds copies after the lexical leg has already ranked them low. It does not change the document frequencies BM25 reads.
- **Limitation:** in ingest mode the harness builds no duplicate clusters. The manifest has no `dup_clusters` input and no trace carries `also_in`. So the ingest de-duplication groups equal the baseline by construction and say nothing about de-duplication. The prechunked result is the evidence.

### 5. `templated_collection` (ingest, 58 measured items)

| Arm | R@5 | R@10 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bm25 | 0.4655 | 0.4655 |
| `ts_rank_cd` | 0.4655 | 0.4828 |

- The R@10 difference is inside the 0.02 band on this branch (bound 0.4628), in every group.
- bm25 does tie more here:
  - 24 items with a near-duplicate tie group, against 15 for `ts_rank_cd`;
  - 61 items with a tie across rank 5, against 38.

  Templated documents share most of their text. The tie inside a group decides which near-identical document reaches the cut. If this slice moves again on a later fixture, ties are the first thing to look at.

## What this means for G1

- **Fixing the fixture loss:** the lever would be IDF over duplicate clusters, counting a cluster once in the document frequency. Collapsing results does not do it. That is a scoring design change for a later plan. This diagnostic proposes it only and builds nothing.
- **Whether the effect matters on real data:** this needs real near-duplicate documents. 04.4-15 Task 3 now has the pass/fail item "real-set near-duplicate coverage". A flip of the default needs PASS there.
- **Decision:** stays `not_flipped`.
