## (b) bm25 on, against v2-default (`ts_rank_cd`): FAIL

| Number | v2-default prechunked | bm25 prechunked | v2-default ingest | bm25 ingest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recall@10 (overall) | 0.7211 | 0.7186 | 0.7511 | 0.7489 |
| MRR (overall) | 0.6370 | 0.6395 | 0.6821 | 0.6821 |
| refusal differences (correct and false, item by item) | — | 0 | — | 0 |
| retrieval total p95 (ms) | — | 68 | — | 82 |

Refusal and latency pass. No overall or language slice moved beyond the noise band. The failing slices:

- `v2-lexical-bm25-on-prechunked`, `category:duplicate_flood`, Recall@5: 0.7500 against 1.0000 (bound 0.9800).
- `v2-lexical-bm25-on-prechunked`, `category:duplicate_flood`, Recall@10: 0.9167 against 1.0000 (bound 0.9800).
- `v2-lexical-bm25-on-ingest`, `category:duplicate_flood`, Recall@5: 0.6667 against 0.9167 (bound 0.8967).
- `v2-lexical-bm25-on-ingest`, `category:duplicate_flood`, Recall@10: 0.8333 against 0.9167 (bound 0.8967).

What the failure looks like:

- The category has 6 synthetic items, 2 per language.
- MRR on it stays 1.0 in every run, so the first hit is still correct.
- What drops is the coverage of the item's other answer spans within the top 5 and top 10. Intent coverage goes from 1.0 to 0.83 (prechunked) and from 0.83 to 0.67 (ingest), and the English pair loses most.

A likely reading, not yet proven: near-duplicate chunks get almost equal BM25 scores and crowd the lexical list. `ts_rank_cd` separated them more. The fixture cannot tell whether this holds on real documents. One move on 6 items is the noise floor (spread 0 against a single reference run), so the real-set intervals decide.

## Benchmark headline (`eval/reports/phase-04.4/benchmark-fixture.md`)

The benchmark ran on PGlite with 30000 neutral chunks. These are ratios only, not budget measurements.

- BM25 takes 1.4 to 3.7 times the `ts_rank_cd` p50, depending on query shape. Rare single-term queries are 29 ms against 8 ms.
- Queries that match most of the corpus hit the candidate cap. There, p95 reaches 891 ms (`mixed_rare_common`) against 594 ms for `ts_rank_cd`.
- The cap holds overlap@10 at 1.0 for every shape except `common_only`, where it is 0.5. That shape has no discriminating term (research R-4). The mean overlap@50 over all benchmark queries is 0.875.

## Parameters

- **k1 = 1.2, b = 0.75** (D-06). These are the Lucene, Elasticsearch and Weaviate defaults, and the pair Phase 7 already pins for Weaviate, so all three backends start from one pair. The owner's offline run used k1 = 1.5. A different default needs the live grid evidence below.
- **Accepted ranges:** k1 0.5 to 3 and b 0 to 1. An invalid value falls back to the default with one warning.
- **Candidate cap:**
  - The app sends `bm25CandidateLimit(n) = min(2000, max(500, 5 × n))` (`src/lib/rag/backend/pgvector.ts`).
  - The SQL accepts `p_candidate_limit` from 0 to 2000, where 0 means uncapped, and defaults to 500 (0904).
  - The lexical limit is 0 to 200.
  - Readiness is cached for 60 s per tenant (`LEXSTATS_READY_TTL_MS`), with at most 1000 entries.
- Document-frequency pruning (D-27) is not built.

## What the live step (plan 04.4-15) must answer

1. **Real-set intervals for the four arms.** Run `python -m rag_eval.lexical_compare` with the arms `lexical_ts_rank_cd`, `rrf_ts_rank_cd`, `lexical_bm25` and `rrf_bm25`, and report paired 95% intervals for Recall@10, Recall@50, MRR and `doc_at_1`, overall and per language. Check in particular whether the fixture's `duplicate_flood` coverage loss shows up on real near-duplicate documents.
2. **The k1 and b grid:** k1 0.9, 1.2 and 1.5; b 0.5 and 0.75.
3. **Real-Postgres timings at 30000, 300000 and 1000000 chunks** against the 400 ms chat and 300 ms voice search budgets, in particular for the shapes that hit the cap.
4. **The capped-versus-uncapped rate on real queries.** How often the cap changes the top 10, and whether `common_only`-like queries are frequent enough to justify the cap lever or D-27 pruning.

Every live action uses the eval project `fsgaeysipkukqoocgzbx` only, never production. Steps: `.planning/phases/04.4-true-bm25-lexical-scoring/handoffs/04.4-13-live-comparison.md`.

## Rule for a later flip

Turning `bm25` on by default is an owner decision on the 04.4-15 evidence. Once `eval/config/gate-population.json` is `real`, it also needs a passing `python -m rag_eval.ship_check`:

- both reports on the real population and the same frozen manifest;
- no slice beyond the noise band;
- the target's paired 95% interval above zero.

Statistics must be built first (`npm run rag:lexstats -- enable --all`, then `check --all`). No tool of this phase writes `eval/config/retrieval-params.json`, `eval/config/refusal-thresholds.json` or a refusal threshold (D-35).
