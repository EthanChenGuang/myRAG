## G1 diagnostic (quick-44, report only)

`eval/reports/phase-04.4/diagnostics/README.md` (tool `python -m rag_eval.phase44_dup_diag`) answers the reading above.

- **Cause:** `duplicate_flood` loses because of BM25 IDF on duplicated text: the flood passage's terms occur in 30 documents, so bm25 scores its copies below unrelated chunks. `ts_rank_cd` has no IDF.
- **Not ties:** score ties are equally common in both arms.
- **Not de-duplication:** `RAG_DUP_CLUSTERS=true`, alone or with `RAG_RESULT_DIVERSITY=collapse`, leaves the prechunked drop unchanged, because collapse acts after the lexical ranking and the document frequencies stay the same. The ingest fixture builds no clusters, so its de-duplication arms are not informative.
- **`templated_collection`:** no drop beyond the band on this branch; bm25 has more near-duplicate ties there.
- **Owner lever (proposed, not built):** cluster-aware document frequency.
- **Status:** stays `not_flipped`. 04.4-15 Task 3 carries the pass/fail item "real-set near-duplicate coverage", which a flip needs.

## Benchmark headline (`eval/reports/phase-04.4/benchmark-fixture.md`)

The benchmark ran on PGlite with 30000 neutral chunks. These are ratios only, not budget measurements.

- BM25 takes 1.4 to 3.7 times the `ts_rank_cd` p50, depending on query shape. Rare single-term queries are 29 ms against 8 ms.
- Queries that match most of the corpus hit the candidate cap. There, p95 reaches 891 ms (`mixed_rare_common`) against 594 ms for `ts_rank_cd`.
- The cap holds overlap@10 at 1.0 for every shape except `common_only`, where it is 0.5. That shape has no discriminating term (research R-4). The mean overlap@50 over all benchmark queries is 0.875.

## Parameters

- **k1 = 1.2, b = 0.75** (D-06). These are the Lucene, Elasticsearch and Weaviate defaults, and the pair Phase 7 already pins for Weaviate, so all three backends start from one pair. The owner's offline run used k1 = 1.5. A different default needs the live grid evidence below.
- **Accepted ranges:** k1 0.5 to 3 and b 0 to 1. An invalid value falls back to the default with one warning.
- **Candidate cap:**
  - The app sends `bm25CandidateLimit(n) = min(2000, max(500, 5 × n))` (`src/lib/rag/backend/pgvector.ts`).
  - The SQL accepts `p_candidate_limit` from 0 to 2000, where 0 means uncapped, and defaults to 500 (0904).
  - The lexical limit is 0 to 200.
  - Readiness is cached for 60 s per tenant (`LEXSTATS_READY_TTL_MS`), with at most 1000 entries.
- Document-frequency pruning (D-27) is not built.

## Owner decision 3A (2026-11-28)

Owner approved in person, 2026-11-28 07:09Z, in the orchestrator session (04.4-CONTEXT D-53a).

- **Finding.** The gate (b) failure on `category:scanned` (ingest, `recall_at_10` 0.9286 < 0.9800, n = 14, one item: `en-scanned-001`) is a boundary flip of the fixture reranker `local_bm25`, not a regression of the BM25 lexical leg. `local_bm25` computes df and avgdl over the rerank candidate pool (pool-relative statistics) and normalises by the pool maximum; the bm25 leg changes 7 of the 75 pool candidates, which moves avgdl (96.77 to 71.23) and df of the one matching query term (8 to 9), and the gold chunk drops from rerank position 8 to 9 (normalised 0.1437 to 0.1357 against 0.1411 for the slot-8 holder).
- **The lexical leg is better for this item.** Lexical rank of the gold chunk 34 to 8, fused rank 8 to 7. With `RAG_RERANKER=none` (ingest, full set) the scanned slice is 14 of 14 in both arms, and the item is rank 8 in the reference and rank 7 with bm25.
- **Evidence.** `.planning/debug/phase44-scanned-slice.md` on branch `origin/debug/44-scanned`, commit 5e637945 (base `origin/exec/04.4-19` 3af3bca9). The same item flipped by the same mechanism in Phase 4.2 (`.planning/debug/phase42-scanned-slice-regression.md`).
- **Consequences.** The gate (b) verdict stays FAIL as measured. The status stays `not_flipped` and the default stays `RAG_LEXICAL_SCORING=ts_rank_cd`. No default, gate, spec, noise band, threshold or golden item is changed, and no number, verdict or hash above is edited. The root fix is plans 04.4-20 to 04.4-22 (plan C of the diagnosis): local reranker statistics from the tenant's lexical statistics, behind `RAG_RERANK_LOCAL_STATS` (default `pool`, today's behaviour); a change of that default needs the owner's signature on the 04.4-22 result (D-57).
