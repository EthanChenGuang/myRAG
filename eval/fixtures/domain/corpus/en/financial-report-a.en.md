## Hollowmere Community Solar Cooperative — Annual Report 2025

Reporting period: 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025
All amounts are in pounds unless stated otherwise.

<!-- chunk -->

## Overview of the year

The cooperative operates five solar arrays owned by 1,312 member households. Total generation reached 8,000 MWh, up from 7,420 MWh in the previous year, because the Tannery Yard canopy ran for its first full year. All electricity was sold under a fixed-price agreement at 130 pounds per MWh.

## Operating result

Revenue of 1,040,000 against operating costs of 292,000 gave an operating surplus of 748,000 before depreciation. The Old Quarry array produced the largest share of output with 3,080 MWh. The Fenwick School roof had the highest operating cost per MWh because its inverter was replaced in the spring.

<!-- chunk -->

## Site performance notes

The Mill Pond array generated 2,615 MWh after a cleaning programme raised panel output by four percent. The Tannery Yard canopy delivered 610 MWh, slightly below its design estimate, because of shading from a neighbouring building during the winter months.

<!-- chunk -->

## Depreciation and loan repayments

Depreciation of the five arrays was 410,000 for the year. The cooperative repaid 215,000 of its community loan, leaving an outstanding balance of 1,385,000 at year end. After depreciation and interest of 64,000, the surplus for the year was 274,000.

<!-- chunk -->

## Outlook

The board proposes a member dividend of 3.5 percent on paid-up shares, payable in May 2026. Planning consent for a sixth array has been requested, and a decision on adding battery storage at the Mill Pond site is expected by the end of 2026.
