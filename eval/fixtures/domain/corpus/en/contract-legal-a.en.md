## River Gauge Data Licence Agreement

Agreement reference: RGL-2025-014
Licensor: Corvalis Hydrometrics Ltd
Licensee: Pellwood Analytics Ltd
Effective date: 3 March 2025

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 1 — Definitions

In this Agreement the following terms have the meanings set out below.
“Gauge Data” means the water level and flow readings recorded by the Licensor's network of 48 river gauges, together with their timestamps and quality flags.
“Derived Output” means any model, chart, index or summary that the Licensee produces from the Gauge Data and from which individual readings cannot be reconstructed.
“Licence Year” means each period of twelve months starting on the effective date.
“Authorised User” means a person working for the Licensee who has accepted the access conditions in Clause 4.
“Release Delay” means the period between the recording of a reading and its delivery to the Licensee.

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 2 — Grant of licence

The Licensor grants the Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Gauge Data for internal research during each Licence Year. The Licensee may publish Derived Output without further consent. Raw Gauge Data must not be published, resold or shared with third parties, except as permitted in Clause 6.

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 3 — Delivery and release delay

The Licensor shall deliver the Gauge Data through a secure file feed every six hours. The standard Release Delay is 36 hours. Readings flagged as provisional may be corrected by the Licensor within 14 days of delivery, and a corrected reading replaces the earlier version.

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 4 — Access conditions

Access to the Gauge Data is limited to a maximum of 25 Authorised Users at any time. Each Authorised User must sign in with an individual account, and shared accounts are not permitted. The Licensee shall keep a register of Authorised Users and provide it to the Licensor within 10 working days of a written request.

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 5 — Fees

The licence fee is 18,400 pounds per Licence Year, payable in advance within 30 days of the start of each Licence Year. Late payment accrues interest at 2 percent per month. If the limit on Authorised Users in Clause 4 is raised, an additional fee of 650 pounds per extra Authorised User applies for the rest of the Licence Year.

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 6 — Emergency sharing

Notwithstanding Clause 2, the Licensee may share raw Gauge Data with a public flood warning authority during a declared flood emergency, provided that the Licensee notifies the Licensor within 48 hours of sharing. During such an emergency the Release Delay set in Clause 3 is reduced to 2 hours at no extra cost.

<!-- chunk -->

## Clause 7 — Term and termination

This Agreement runs for three Licence Years. Either party may terminate it on 90 days' written notice. On termination the Licensee shall delete all raw Gauge Data within 30 days, but may keep any Derived Output created before the termination date.
