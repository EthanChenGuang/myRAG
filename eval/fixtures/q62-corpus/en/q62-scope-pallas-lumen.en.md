## Sensor array calibration cadence

In Zone Pallas the Lumen unit recalibrates each sensor array every 21 days. A recalibration takes 55 minutes and is logged in the array register.

<!-- chunk -->

## Spare filter stock

The Lumen unit in Zone Pallas keeps 8 spare filter cartridges on hand. A new order is placed when fewer than 3 cartridges remain.
