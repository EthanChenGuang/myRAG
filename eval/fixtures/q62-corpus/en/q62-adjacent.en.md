## Snowfield temperature logging

Temperature probes on the snowfield are read twice a day, at 07:00 and at 19:00. Each reading is copied into the field notebook within one hour.

<!-- chunk -->

## Probe battery swaps

A snowfield probe battery is swapped every 30 days, or sooner when the probe reports a voltage below 3.1 volts.
