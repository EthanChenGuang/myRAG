## Sensor array calibration cadence

In Zone Oriel the Corvid unit recalibrates each sensor array every 14 days. A recalibration takes 40 minutes and is logged in the array register.

## Spare filter stock

The Corvid unit in Zone Oriel keeps 12 spare filter cartridges on hand. A new order is placed when fewer than 4 cartridges remain.
