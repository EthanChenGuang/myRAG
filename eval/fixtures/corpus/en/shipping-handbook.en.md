## Carrier cut-off times

Lumen Valley Instruments ships from one warehouse. Orders paid before 14:00 on a business day leave the warehouse the same day with the standard carrier. Orders paid after 14:00 leave on the next business day. The express carrier has an earlier cut-off of 12:00 for same-day dispatch. Orders placed on a Saturday or Sunday leave on Monday.

<!-- chunk -->

## Packaging rules

Every instrument is packed in a foam-lined box with a moisture sachet. Thermal probes with a cable longer than 3 metres are coiled and fixed with a paper band before boxing. Packages that weigh more than 20 kilograms are shipped on a pallet and are never sent as ordinary parcels. Batteries are packed separately from instruments and carry a lithium handling label when the model uses lithium cells.

<!-- chunk -->

## Delivery times by region

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the home country, 5 to 8 business days within the neighbouring region and 8 to 14 business days for all other destinations. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days at home and 3 to 4 business days in the neighbouring region. Delivery times count from dispatch, not from the order date.

<!-- chunk -->

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free for orders above 250 dollars. Below that amount a flat fee of 12 dollars applies. Express shipping always costs 35 dollars. Pallet shipments are quoted by the logistics team before payment, and the quote stays valid for 7 days.

<!-- chunk -->

## Customs and import duties

Orders to destinations outside the home country may be subject to import duties and taxes that are charged by the customs authority of the destination. Lumen Valley Instruments does not collect these amounts. Each parcel carries a commercial invoice that lists the SKU, the declared value and the country of origin of every item.

<!-- chunk -->

## Damaged or lost parcels

Report a damaged parcel within 5 days of delivery and keep the outer box and the foam for inspection. Write to logistics@example.com with the order number and two photos. A parcel that has not arrived 15 business days after dispatch is treated as lost, and the logistics team either sends a replacement or hands the case to the billing team for a refund.
