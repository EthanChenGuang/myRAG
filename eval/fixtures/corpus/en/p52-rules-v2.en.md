# Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch rules v2

These launch rules of the Marrowfield Rocketry Club apply from 1 March 2026.

<!-- chunk -->

## Launch window

On every Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch day the launch window closes at 19:15, and no rocket lifts off after that time.

<!-- chunk -->

## Motor size

Marrowfield Rocketry Club members may fly motors with a total impulse of up to 80 newton-seconds.

<!-- chunk -->

## Spectator line

At Marrowfield Rocketry Club launches, spectators stand at least 20 metres behind the launch pads.

<!-- chunk -->

## Season registration

Registration for the Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch season closes on 2026-04-12.

<!-- chunk -->

## Launch day crew

A Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch day needs 2 pad marshals on duty, and every rocket must carry 5 metres of recovery cord.
