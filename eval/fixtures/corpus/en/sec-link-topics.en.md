## Wrenfield WB-3 bird bath heater

The Wrenfield WB-3 bird bath heater is a flat disc that sits in the basin and keeps the water open down to minus 15 degrees Celsius. It draws 60 watts. Assembly pictures are at https://help.fixture-site.example/wrenfield-wb3.

## Cobaltine CS-9 calligraphy pen set

The Cobaltine CS-9 calligraphy pen set holds a walnut holder and 9 steel nibs from 1 to 5 millimetres wide. A printed practice sheet is at https://help.fixture-site.example/cobaltine-cs9.

## Ashgrove AT-5 star tracker

The Ashgrove AT-5 star tracker follows the sky for up to 3 hours on four AA cells and carries cameras of up to 2 kilograms. The polar alignment guide is at https://help.fixture-site.example/ashgrove-at5.

## Dunmore DM-6 metronome

The Dunmore DM-6 metronome ticks from 30 to 250 beats per minute and has a brass pendulum with a sliding weight. A tempo chart is at https://help.fixture-site.example/dunmore-dm6.
