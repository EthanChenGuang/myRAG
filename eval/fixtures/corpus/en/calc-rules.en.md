## Talvoran tally rule A: cap on each lot

Under Talvoran tally rule A, each lot counts for at most 40 pell. A lot recorded above 40 pell counts as 40 pell, and the counted lots are then added together.

<!-- chunk -->

## Talvoran tally rule B: threshold for each round

Under Talvoran tally rule B, only the part of a round above 120 pell is counted. A round of 120 pell or less counts as 0 pell, and the counted parts of all rounds are added together.

<!-- chunk -->

## Talvoran tally rule C: added share

Under Talvoran tally rule C, the batches of one tally are added together and the sum gains an added share of 15% of itself. The counted size is the sum plus that share.

<!-- chunk -->

## Talvoran tally rule D: tiered rate per interval

Under Talvoran tally rule D, each interval is charged 2 pell per step for its first 50 steps and 3 pell per step for every further step past 50. The charges of all intervals are added together.

<!-- chunk -->

## Talvoran tally rule E: length of an interval

Under Talvoran tally rule E, an interval is counted in days from its starting date to its closing date, and both the starting day and the closing day count. Each counted day adds 4 pell.
