## XR-4410 thermal probe

The XR-4410 thermal probe (SKU LV-77-0192) is a handheld probe for lab benches. Measurement range is -40 to 250 degrees Celsius. Accuracy is plus or minus 0.5 degrees Celsius. Response time is 2 seconds. The probe cable is 3 metres long. Power comes from two AA batteries. The housing is rated IP54. The firmware build shipped with this model is FW-2.8.14.

## Firmware and compatibility

Firmware build FW-2.8.14 supports the XR-4410, the XR-4420 and the XR-5100. The DL-220 data logger uses its own firmware line, DLF-1.3.2. Device identifiers have the form LVI-XR-000123, where the middle part is the model family and the last six digits are the serial number. To update a probe, connect it over USB-C and run the Lumen Link desktop tool, which is available at https://example.com/downloads/lumen-link.

## Warranty and replacement parts

Every instrument in this list carries a two year limited warranty that starts on the delivery date.
