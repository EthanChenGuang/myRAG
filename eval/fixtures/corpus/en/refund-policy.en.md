## Annual plans

Lumen Valley Instruments accepts refund requests for plans billed once a year. Send the request to the billing team with your order number. The refund is paid to the original payment method within 10 business days. The annual plans refund window is 45 days from the purchase date.

<!-- chunk -->

## Monthly plans

Lumen Valley Instruments accepts refund requests for plans billed once a month. Send the request to the billing team with your order number. The refund is paid to the original payment method within 10 business days. The monthly plans refund window is 14 days from the purchase date.

<!-- chunk -->

## Exceptions

Custom calibration services and devices returned with physical damage are not eligible for a refund. Contact the support team to discuss other exceptions.

<!-- chunk -->

## Previous policy (2023)

Until the end of 2023, Lumen Valley Instruments used an earlier refund rule. Any customer could ask for their money back within 30 days of buying a plan, whatever the billing period, and approved refunds were issued as store credit instead of being returned to the original payment method. This earlier rule was retired on 1 January 2024 and no longer applies to new orders.

<!-- chunk -->

## How to submit a request

Write to refunds@example.com with your order number and the email address used at checkout. Phone requests are handled on 555-0142 between 9:00 and 17:00 on weekdays. A refund request is acknowledged within two business days.
