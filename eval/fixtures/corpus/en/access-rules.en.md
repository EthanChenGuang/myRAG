## Extended loan period

A certified operator is a borrower who has passed the handling exam. Items borrowed by a certified operator may be kept for 30 days. Every other borrower returns borrowed items within 10 days.

<!-- chunk -->

## Late return fee

A partner lab is a lab listed in Annex 2 of this guide. Borrowers from a partner lab pay no late return fee. Every other borrower pays a late return fee of 4 credits per day.

<!-- chunk -->

## Shipping of bulk orders

A bulk order is an order of more than 50 units. Bulk orders are shipped to the requester within 5 working days. Smaller orders are collected at the front desk.

<!-- chunk -->

## Night access to the store room

A night key card is given to anyone who has completed the safety walk-through. Holders of a night key card may enter the store room between 20:00 and 06:00. Without a night key card the store room is open from 08:00 to 18:00 only.
