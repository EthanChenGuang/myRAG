## Pinecone Community Library lending rules

The Pinecone Community Library lends to cardholders. Each library card may have at most 7 books on loan at once, each loan lasts 21 days, and a loan can be renewed once at the self-service kiosk. Picture books stay in the reading room and cannot be taken home.
