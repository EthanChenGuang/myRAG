# Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch rules v1

These launch rules of the Marrowfield Rocketry Club apply from 1 September 2025.

<!-- chunk -->

## Launch window

On every Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch day the launch window closes at 18:30, and no rocket lifts off after that time.

<!-- chunk -->

## Motor size

Marrowfield Rocketry Club members may fly motors with a total impulse of up to 40 newton-seconds.

<!-- chunk -->

## Spectator line

At Marrowfield Rocketry Club launches, spectators stand at least 15 metres behind the launch pads.

<!-- chunk -->

## Season registration

Registration for the Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch season closes on 2025-10-05.

<!-- chunk -->

## Launch day crew

Each Marrowfield Rocketry Club launch day needs 1 pad marshal on duty, and every rocket must carry 3 metres of recovery cord.
