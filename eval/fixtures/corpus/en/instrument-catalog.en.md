## Flow meter QF-410

The flow meter QF-410 (item code QC-41-0007) measures liquid flow from 0.5 to 40 litres per minute. It weighs 610 grams and runs on two AA batteries.

## Pressure gauge QP-220

The pressure gauge QP-220 (item code QC-22-0013) reads pressure from 0 to 16 bar with an accuracy of 1 percent. Its housing is made of stainless steel.

## Sound level meter QS-90

The sound level meter QS-90 (item code QC-09-0021) measures from 30 to 130 decibels. It stores up to 2,000 readings and connects over USB-C.

## Light meter QL-55

The light meter QL-55 (item code QC-55-0034) measures from 1 to 200,000 lux. Its display shows readings with one decimal place.
