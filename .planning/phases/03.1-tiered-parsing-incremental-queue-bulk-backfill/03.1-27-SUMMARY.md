- `IngestWorker` claims with reserved non-batch slot, 2s jittered poll, wake, 40s heartbeat, graceful shutdown, and `[Ingest Alert]` on terminal failures.
- `PriorityPool` orders backfill vs upload (priority 100) with FIFO within priority.
- `RAG_INGEST_WORKER=on` starts the worker in FastAPI lifespan; `/v1/ingest/wake` returns `{woken}`; `/ready` surfaces `worker_env_missing` and `worker_running`.
- `SidecarSettings` refuses recorded converter paths unless `RAG_SIDECAR_ENV=test`.
