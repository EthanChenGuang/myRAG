## Accomplishments

- **Routing and forced tiers (Task 1).** `buildIngestFixtureDatabase({ routing })` starts the app's parse-external and advance routes behind a local server, points the object store, tier bounds and ledger reads at the build's own edge, builds the job snapshot from an explicit queue config, then per document calls `enqueueIngest` and runs `rag_sidecar.ingest.run_once` through `spawnSidecarModule` (recorded converter), one job at a time in upload order. The recorded dir is a temp folder: parse recordings as they are, typed twins mapped to the parse fixture's pages, other typed fixtures with their whole-document recording for every preset and page. `--force-tier premium` answers the fake LlamaParse from the recorded premium pages, else from the structured page rendered as markdown. run-smoke parses the flags (`parseIngestRoutingFlags`) and threads them through runEval, prepareInProcessEval and createFixtureDatabase.
- **Corpus addition.** The default ingest mode now also ingests cross-boundary, digital-text, mixed-routing, multi-column-en and table-en (the scans and invoice-en are their typed twins). The 138 pre-existing items score exactly as before.
- **Golden items (Task 2).** 27 answerable items (3 per class) plus `parse-no-answer-{en,zh,it}`, `review_status: approved`, `provenance: synthetic_fixture`, tagged `parse_fixtures`, `parse_class:<class>`, `parse_fixture:<id>`. All 27 hit at k=5 in the inline run.
- **Re-baseline and Recall@k (Task 3).** `python -m rag_eval.baseline record --runs 3 --fixture-mode ingest` wrote 168 items (deterministic, spread 0). `parser_eval --recall-runs fast=... structured=... premium=...` fills `recall_at_k` per tier and per fixture and adds `recall_runs` and `recall_by_class` to the details; `recall_at_k_measured` is true.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Premium page texts lost every paragraph after the first**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (forced premium: the scan's page held only its heading and failed `low_chars`)
- **Issue:** `pageTexts` in `callbacks/premium.ts` split the joined LlamaParse markdown on blank lines and gave sub-page N the N-th paragraph, so any page with more than one paragraph was cut and later pages got the wrong text. Real LlamaParse pages almost always have several paragraphs.
- **Fix:** the LlamaParse parser returns its per-page text with the done attempt (`pages`, failed pages empty); the callback maps those to original page numbers and keeps the old split only for a parser without pages.
- **Files:** src/lib/rag/ingest/parsers/shared.ts, llamaparse-v2.ts, callbacks/premium.ts, tests/rag/ingest/parse-external.test.ts
- **Committed in:** 8b1a6de4, 69f07164

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Recorded fast pages of scanned fixtures did not load (deferred item 4)**
- **Issue:** `--force-tier fast` reaches these pages; `body.children` pointed at removed texts.
- **Fix:** `build_recorded.py` clears `body.children` for scanned fast pages; four recordings regenerated (the run reproduces every other file byte for byte).
- **Committed in:** 09fd0568

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] multi-column-en had no column text (deferred item 5, second bullet)**
- **Issue:** the recorder skipped `ColumnParagraph`, so the multi-column class had one indexed sentence and nothing to ask about.
- **Fix:** `record_parse_results.py` emits both columns, left first; four page-2 recordings regenerated. The parser report's structured text and order scores rise to 1.0 as a result.
- **Committed in:** 09fd0568

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Harness gaps for the queued build**

- **Committed in:** 16da1ceb

**5. [Scope] Files beyond `files_modified`**
