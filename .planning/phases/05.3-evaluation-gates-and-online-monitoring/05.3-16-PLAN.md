<objective>
Make the absolute gates part of every CI tier, enforced for the tiers that the measurement shows passing, with a negative control for the absolute rule (D-06; orchestrator decision O-1).

Purpose: a model or prompt that answers what it should refuse fails CI even when the noise band passes (EVAL-22); an unversioned golden change fails pr-smoke (EVAL-29).
Output: harness default, workflow steps, lint rules and lint tests.

Orchestrator decision O-1: `--gates` is enforced in CI only for tiers that pass; a tier marked `report` in the measurement record runs report-only until the owner ratifies it (H-10 in plan 05.3-50). Orchestrator decision O-2: untested nightly floors start report-only (floors bind only for gating calibration cells; the nightly schedule runs its absolute gate in report mode until H-8).
