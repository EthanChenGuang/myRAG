## Contracts

- **GoldenItem.attack** (`AttackSpec`): `canary` is 6 to 128 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9._:-]`. `injected_quotes` holds at least one GoldSpan. `forbidden_claims` holds at least one non-blank literal string, never a regex. Validators, each with an error message that names its rule:
  - `attack` is present exactly for `injection` and `injection_evasive`;
  - injection items must be answerable (checked first, so this error wins over the generic no_answer rule);
  - injected quotes contain no `<` or `>`;
  - the canary, after NFKC and case-folding, is absent from `expected_answer` and from every gold quote;
  - no injected document is a gold document.
- **RagTrace.injection** (`TraceInjection`): `mode` is `flag`, `drop` or `downweight` (no record when the check is off); `checked` is a list of `{chunk_id, document}`; `flagged` is a list of `{chunk_id, document, detectors (min 1), rule_ids}`; `dropped`, `downweighted` and `detectors_unavailable` are lists of ids or names. `refusal_reason` accepts `injection_filtered`.
- **TraceEffectiveConfig.security**: an optional `{rules_version, detectors_version}`.
- **EvalReport.injection** (`InjectionReport`): `config_label` is `all_off`, `recommended`, `tagging_only` or `other`; `items`; three MetricValue maps keyed by slice name (`overall`, `language:<l>`, `category:<c>`, and `<detector>:<language>` for false positives); `rules_false_positive_count`; and `gate`, which is `absolute_zero` (recommended, A-13) or `report_only`.
- All new fields are optional or defaulted. Every line in eval/fixtures/golden.jsonl, plus the existing trace and report samples, still validates.

## Behaviour changes

- None at runtime with every switch at its default. The eval route still rejects unknown stage names with a 400. It now also accepts `injection_check` in `disabled_stages`, and passes only stages the TS registry (`CORE_STAGES` + `OPTIONAL_STAGES`) knows to the pipeline. Until 05.1-06 registers `injection_check`, disabling it is a no-op, which is right because the stage does not exist yet. Once 05.1-06 lands, it is forwarded with no further change.
- Parsed traces in TS now carry `injection: null` and `effective_config.security: null` defaults. `config_hash` is unchanged, and both test-rag runs pass their gates.

## Deviations

1. **Four more regenerated schema files**: Baseline, DraftedItemBatch, JudgeReport and RunManifest. They embed `Category` or `RagRunOptions`, so `gen:schemas` changes them, and committing them is required for a clean diff. They were not edited by hand.
2. **Eval route type narrowing (Rule 1/3, blocking).** Adding `injection_check` to the contract `StageName` broke `tsc` in `src/app/api/internal/rag/run/route.ts`. The TS stage registry `src/lib/rag/config.ts` belongs to the parallel plan 05.1-06. To avoid a merge conflict there, the route filters `disabled_stages` through the registry. The fix has its own test, which failed before the fix.
3. The plan left the report's `gate` field open. It is `absolute_zero | report_only`, matching A-13 and the 05.1-10 gate kind.
4. `DraftedItemProposal.category` shares `Category`, so a draft proposal may name the injection categories, but it has no `attack` field. Such a proposal fails `GoldenItem` validation on promotion, which fails closed. This is left as is.
