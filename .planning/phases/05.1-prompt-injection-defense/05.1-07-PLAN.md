<objective>
Add the Prompt Guard classification endpoint to the sidecar with windowing and an injectable scorer, plus the app-side client that turns every failure into a value.

Purpose: D-08 (sidecar endpoint, Pydantic contracts, shared-secret auth that fails closed, windowed maximum), D-15 (a deterministic fake endpoint for tests; no live calls), D-09 (failures are values, never refusals). The real model loader, the optional extra and the image target need the license and gated-access checkpoint and come in plan 05.1-18; the stage wiring is plan 05.1-21.
Output: the sidecar injection package (router, scorer protocol and fake, windowing), the router registration and settings, the TypeScript client, tests on both sides.

Runs after Phase 5 is complete. Relies on: plans 05.1-03 (contracts and generated Zod), 05.1-04 (switches), 05-12 (last edits to main.py and settings.py, and the router-per-feature layout of the groundedness package). Rollout position: D-19 step 7.
