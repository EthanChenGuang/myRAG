## Behaviour changes

- Fixture data only. No app code changed, and no switch was added.
- Prechunked mode inserts the six new documents like every other corpus document. The insert loop moved into `prechunkedWriter`; the rows written are unchanged.
- Ingest mode sends every corpus document except `sec-attacks.<lang>.md` through `ingest()`, as before. It writes the three attack documents through `prechunkedWriter` in corpus order and then builds their lexical text with `relexFixtureChunks`. Without this, `ingest()` under `RAG_INJECTION_SCAN=quarantine` would quarantine the rule-caught attacks, and the build would stop with "ended quarantined (expected ready)".
