## Behaviour changes

- **Calc check (all channels, only with `RAG_CALC_CHECK` on):** a wrong total equal to an operand or an intermediate is now corrected, not reported verified or `stated_not_found`. Example: a cap applied to the sum (stated 40, the cap operand; recomputed 105) was `verified` before, because the locator skipped 40 and fell back to a listed step value. A total equal to the base sum (400; recomputed 460) was `stated_not_found`. The eval observation (`calc_observed.stated`) uses the same rule, so the check-off run now records the wrong totals. With the check off, nothing changes in chat, embed, voice or eval answers.
- **Eval report:** `report.json` gains `calc`, `overflow` and `run_config_name`, each only when present. `calc` appears on any run that generated answers for the multi_step_calc items, the default smoke tiers included (report-only there). `overflow` appears only with `RAG_PROMPT_BUDGET` on, and `run_config_name` only with a run config. `report.md` gains the "Calculation (Phase 5.2.1)" and "Prompt budget overflow (Phase 5.2.1)" sections next to the answer-rule rows. Items, slices, the baseline comparison and the paired block are unchanged.
- **Gate:** for the seven run configs only, Phase 5.2.1 offenders join the noise-band and Phase 5.1/5.2 offenders, and gate.md gets a section listing every check. Every other run is gated exactly as before.
- **CI:** pr-smoke has the new job `eval-smoke-p521` (needed by the aggregate) and a `paired.all_zero` step after the all-off smoke tier. Nightly has the new job `calc-live`, behind `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`.
- Strict "information not available", webSearch with Exa, `source_images`, `{{context}}`, voice and embed are unchanged.

### Plan line 110: budget-overflow gates (not relaxed)

`overflow_error_rate` is 0 in both pipelines: 0 of 12 `answer_error`, and the fake was never exceeded and never saw the question out of last place. With the quick-packing-budget-routes merge this part holds, as the orchestrator expected. `history_before_documents_rate` and `budget_trace_rate` are 1 in both pipelines. **`must_keep_rate` is not 1, so both gates fail.** Per item:

| Pipeline | Item | must_keep | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| legacy | en/zh/it `-001`, `-002`, `-003` (9 items) | 0 | Refused at strict retrieval before any packing. The same 9 items are refused in a budget-off legacy run (calc-tool-follow traces), so this is the legacy relevance rule on these fixtures, not the budget. |
| v2 | `en-context-overflow-002`, `zh-context-overflow-002` | 1 of 2 spans | The two must-keep parents are ranks 1 and 2 at 899 and 865 raw tokens. The v2 packing budget is 1,785 raw tokens. With the assembly framing counted (quick 261127-pbr), only rank 1 fits, and 7 documents are dropped. |
| v2 | `it-context-overflow-002` | 0 of 2 spans | The must-keep parents are retrieval ranks 3 and 4 (1,150 and 1,147 tokens). Ranks 1 and 2 are kept. |
| v2 | `it-context-overflow-003` | 0 of 1 | The must-keep parent is rank 2. Rank 1 (1,143 tokens) fills the budget. |

In v2 the packer keeps the highest-ranked parents that fit, which is D-88's property. The gold `must_keep` sets do not match that under the fixture embedder and the 4,096-token fake window once framing is counted. Fixing this needs either the fixture items (plan 12's context_overflow items or the fake window, with a golden-set bump and a baseline re-record) or a packing change (truncating the last kept parent). Both are outside this plan, and the choice is the owner's. The two pr-smoke steps for these runs will stay red until that is done.
