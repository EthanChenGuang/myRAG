# Phase 5.2.1 Plan 14: Calculation offer in the chat route

**Behind `RAG_CALC_TOOL`, the chat route now offers the calculation on the turns that need it: as the `calculate` tool when the turn can use tools, or as the trailing-block instruction and transform when it cannot (the default model). The tool runs server-side without approval, calculation turns stay on the chunk-alias citation path, and channels that do not render citations still get block aliases with the markers stripped from what they display. WhatsApp reaches all of this through `/api/chat` with no change of its own.**
