<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Two deliverables, both off by default:

- **Calculation tool and check (GEN-16, GEN-17, EVAL-26):** a deterministic `calculate` tool in the chat tool set that evaluates a structured calculation in exact decimal arithmetic and never runs free-form code; a server-side check that recomputes every computed number an answer states, verifies each operand against its cited block or the question, and adds a correction or "not verified" notice without rewriting streamed text; the `multi_step_calc` fixture category with paraphrase groups.
- **Whole-prompt token budget (RNK-08, EVAL-33):** the document budget is computed from the model's context window minus every other part of the prompt; history is trimmed before documents; the question is always present and last; an optional several-call mode for evidence that still does not fit; the `context_overflow` fixture items with a small-window fake model.

ROADMAP success criteria 8 and 9 of "Phase 5.2: Grounded Answers & Output Safety" are this phase's criteria.

**Depends on Phase 5.2** (all of it runs first):
- the text registry reader `fixedText` and the keys `notices.calc_corrected`, `notices.calc_not_verified`, `instructions.calculation` (05.2 D-08);
- the answer-rule composer `composeAnswerRules` and its fixed order, in which the calculation instruction is part 6 (05.2 D-04, D-05);
- the notice emitter `emitNotice` (05.2 D-11);
- the `rag_answer_records.checks` column and the `AnswerChecks` contract (05.2 D-23, migration 0940);
- the output cap's effective value as the answer reserve (05.2 D-31);
- the review reasons and the two routes into the review queue (05.2 D-55);
- the golden contract categories `multi_step_calc` and `context_overflow` (05.2 D-53) and the fake-model harness pattern (05.2 D-54);
- the Config UI panel "Answer rules and output safety" (05.2 D-57) and the config reader (05.2 D-58).

**Depends on earlier phases:**
- Phase 5: the per-request `AliasMap` and the rule that aliases are assigned after the last context step (05 D-02), the marker parser and stream transforms (05 D-04), Claude path eligibility for tool turns (05 D-10), `rag_answer_records` and its read route (05 D-15 to D-17).
- Phase 5.1: the `<documents>` wrapping and notice (05.1 D-01 to D-04), `retrievalConfigHash` covering answer-affecting switches (05.1 D-18).
- Phase 5.1.1: the review queue `rag_review_items` and its mining function (05.1.1 D-13 to D-15).
- Phase 4: the v2 context budget `RAG_CONTEXT_TOKEN_BUDGET` and `selectWithinBudget` (04 D-16), parent expansion.
- Phase 3.2: the language registry, which gains the `number_format` field here (03.2 D-04).
- Phase 2: `assembleContext` shared by chat, embed and voice (02 D-16), the voice scope token (02, PIPE-04).

Out of this phase: everything else of Phase 5.2; fact tools as operand sources (SEC-05 is a design only); a unit or currency table; version-aware retrieval.

### Calculation check (GEN-17)

- **D-73:** Carried whole. `RAG_CALC_CHECK`; the check runs at stream end, before `finish`.

- **D-77:** Carried whole. `multi_step_calc` items with paraphrase groups; `numeric_correctness` and `paraphrase_consistency`.

### Whole-prompt token budget (RNK-08)

- **D-79:** Carried whole. `RAG_PROMPT_BUDGET`; one planner used by chat, embed and voice-rag.
- **D-80:** Carried whole. The budget formula; the window from `contextLength`; the answer reserve; `config/rag/prompt-budget.json`.
- **D-81:** Carried whole. Counting with `tiktoken` times a calibrated per-family factor plus a margin; `eval/config/tokenizer-calibration.json`.
- **D-82:** Carried whole. History trimmed oldest-first before any document; the question never trimmed and always last.
- **D-83:** Carried whole. Parent dedupe and rank-order packing in both the v2 and the legacy assembly; aliases after packing.
- **D-84:** Carried whole. No provider-side truncation; the structured `prompt_budget_exceeded` error.
- **D-85:** Carried whole. `RAG_CONTEXT_OVERFLOW=truncate|refine`; `refine` is its own optional plan, last in the phase, and may be moved to the backlog by the plan checker without affecting success criterion 9.
- **D-86:** Carried whole. Trace fields and the `checks.prompt_budget` summary.
- **D-87:** Carried whole. Voice: optional `modelContextLength` and `historyTokens` in the voice-rag request, otherwise the fixed voice budget.
