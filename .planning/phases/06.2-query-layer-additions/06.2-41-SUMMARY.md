  - The voice baseline was re-recorded in Task 3.
- **Finding: in prechunked mode no fixture tenant had any KB language shares.**
  - The prechunked writer stores a document's language only in `metadata.language`. It never writes the `documents.language` column that `rag_tenant_language_shares` counts.
  - So the RPC returns no rows for the main and q62 tenants. The gate's `translateNeeded` then sees no known share and never raises R8.
  - This, not "every language above 20%", is why R8 fired for 0 of 356 items in 06.2-27's prechunked voice arms.
  - Ingest mode detects languages at ingestion. There the main tenant has en 0.355, zh 0.325 and it 0.319, which is 06.2-22's explanation.
  - This plan records `documents.language` for the dominant tenant only. The main and q62 tenants are unchanged, so no baseline moves.

## Task 2: tenant, loader, routing, R8 assertion

### Corpus (described by structure)

- **Eight zh documents**, two sections each. Every document states the maintenance or operating facts of one instrument at one invented field station: an interval, a threshold, a time, a capacity. The station is named only in Chinese, so en and it questions share no tokens with these documents, and only a translated variant can reach them.
- **One en document and one it document** on unrelated equipment topics. Nothing asks about them; they exist so that both languages hold a share of 0.1.
- The manifest has no scope vocabulary and no glossary rows.
- No REQUIREMENTS example domain appears. The names do not collide with the main, q62 or ingest corpora (checked with grep). `test_fixture_pii` scans the 10 documents and the manifest.

### Loader and routing

- The 06.2-34 functions now take an `IsolatedTenantDescriptor`. The q62 exports keep their signatures and behaviour; the dominant tenant is a second descriptor.
- The owner goes into `admin_users` only when the tenant has a scope vocabulary, which leaves q62 as before.
- New option `dominantLanguage` on `createFixtureDatabase`, `buildIngestFixtureDatabase` and `prepareInProcessEval`. The corpus hash includes the dominant corpus and manifest hashes only when the tenant is loaded.
- `runEval` builds the tenant only when the run holds tagged items, and runs those items last. Each one runs against its own document id map in ingest mode and its own scope in multi-tenant mode.
- The harness resets the KB language-share cache on each switch, through `withIsolatedTenant(..., onSwitch)` and `InProcessEval.resetTenantCaches`. Without it, a single-tenant run would serve the dominant tenant the main tenant's cached `s:default` shares.

### Shares and R8

- `rag_tenant_language_shares`: zh 0.8, en 0.1, it 0.1 in prechunked mode (single- and multi-tenant) and in ingest mode.
- `translationTargets`: en → zh, it → zh, zh → none.
- With `RAG_PIPELINE=v2` and `RAG_QUERY_LAYER=true`, en and it probes raise `R8_LANGUAGE_MISMATCH`, list `translate` and run it (stage `ok`). The zh probe raises neither.
- This holds through the text entry (single- and multi-tenant) and through the voice entry when `RAG_QUERY_VOICE_TRANSFORMS` lists translate. With the default voice list, translate never runs.

**Finding for 06.2-30: translate fires, but the strict similarity floor still refuses.**

- In the translate arm, and in the layer-on text entry, 5 of the 6 cross-language items end `refused` with `below_similarity_floor`.
- Their `refusal.top_score` and the candidates' `similarity` are the original query's cosine against the zh chunks: about 0.02–0.03 for en, about 0.11–0.13 for it. The candidates the translated variant brings in rank high on fused score.
- So the floor appears to judge the untranslated query's similarity, which cancels the effect of translation under strict mode.
- This plan changes no retrieval code or default. The behaviour is recorded here for the 06.2-30 comparison and its eligibility decision.
