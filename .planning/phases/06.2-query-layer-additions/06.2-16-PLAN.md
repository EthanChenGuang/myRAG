<objective>
Search each filtered sub-question with its narrowed filter, fall back once without it when nothing comes back, and keep every search inside the cap of eight.

Purpose: D-08 (per-variant filter on the Phase 4 variant search input, one unfiltered retry, cap accounting, fusion and rerank unchanged) on top of D-06 narrowing from 06.2-11.
Output: plan-to-variant mapping, the search-stage change, a unit test and a PGlite two-tenant and subset test.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: the shared retrieval filters (base) with an optional per-variant narrowed copy. Decision: add-alongside — QueryVariant gains an optional filters field; absent means the shared base. Rationale: D-06 keeps the shared filters as the base and allows variants only to narrow; promoting every variant to carry its own filter would touch every Phase 4 and 6 variant consumer for no behavioural gain. Accepted debt: if a later phase gives every variant its own filter, the per-variant field becomes primary and the shared filter a default.
</assumption_delta_decision>
