## 06.2-41 finding 4: the similarity floor after translate (not covered by this plan)
- **Reproduced.** In the text single-pass arm and in the voice-translate arm, translate fires on all 6 dominant-language items (variants `original` + `translated`, status `ok`). 5 of them still end `refused` / `below_similarity_floor`. Their `top_score` is the original query's cosine: about 0.02–0.03 for en and 0.11–0.13 for it.
- **Root cause.** `floorSimilarity` in `src/lib/rag/v2/stages/rank.ts:215` reads `row.standaloneSimilarity` under the query layer. That is deliberate and documented: D-06 says "the floor reads the standalone variant's similarity, never a HyDE, expanded or translated list". This is a locked design decision, not a bug.
- **Why I did not change it.** Changing it changes when strict mode answers versus says "information not available", which the core value protects. It also needs an owner decision on D-06, and this plan only runs measurements. The plan does not cover the finding. I changed nothing and am reporting it.
- **Recommendation (owner decision, then a gap plan).** Under the query layer, let the floor take the larger of the standalone similarity and the translated variant's similarity. A translation is a faithful rendering of the query, unlike HyDE or expand. Gate the change on:
  - the false-answer rate of unanswerable cross_lingual items;
  - a refusal recalibration.

  Then rerun the voice arms.
