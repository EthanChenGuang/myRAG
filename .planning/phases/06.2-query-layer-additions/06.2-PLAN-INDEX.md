## Execution order and cross-phase dependencies

- **Roadmap order.** Phase 6.2 depends on Phases 6, 6.1, 3.2, 4.2 and 5.2 (and through them on 3.1.1, 4.1, 4.1.1 and 5.1.1). Cross-phase edges do not change wave numbers (same convention as 06-PLAN-INDEX.md).
- **D-01 preconditions.** Every plan that consumes a foreign seam opens with a precondition task (files: none) that checks the owning plan's SUMMARY file and the seam's symbol, column, function or file, records the names actually found (owners may rename; consume by symbol and re-read the newest definition) and stops with a blocker naming the owning phase and plan when the capability is absent. No plan stubs another phase's deliverable in `src/`; fakes live only under `tests/` and `eval/fixtures/`.
- **Phase 4.2 (no plans yet).** Only **06.2-25** (RET-11 `versions` in sub-question filters, its cache bypass, the LANG-07 glossary merge test) and **06.2-31** (`version_compare` golden slice and the decision-record update) wait for it; their preconditions find the delivering 4.2 plans by requirement id in the 4.2 SUMMARY files. 06.2-26 reads the RNK-07 per-mode targets when present and the single target otherwise (P-01), so QRY-14 does not wait. The phase close (06.2-32) and live verification (06.2-33) come after 06.2-31 and therefore wait for 4.2.
- **Phase 5.2 (no plans yet).** **06.2-09** needs the GEN-08 "documents do not cover it" text (D-12) and stops without it; through the answer-path chain this gates 06.2-23, 06.2-29, 06.2-30 and later.
