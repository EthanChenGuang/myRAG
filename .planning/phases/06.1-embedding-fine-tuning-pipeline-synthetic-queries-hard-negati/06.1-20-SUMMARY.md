# 06.1-20 Summary: fine-tuned slots through 3.1.1 bindings

A fine-tuned slot can now be scoped to doc types and served through Phase 3.1.1 bindings. Migration 0654 keeps a slot inside its consent at the binding layer, in the re-embed runs and on taint. A tenant-private model can serve only its own tenant. No sequence of service-role calls serves it globally, binds it deployment-wide or to another tenant, or re-embeds another tenant's chunks with it.

## Behaviour changes

- `ft-bind <slot> <version> --doc-types <docType,...|all>` binds and scopes in one SQL call. Without the flag the behaviour is unchanged (unscoped slot, global EMB-05 path). A tenant-scope version without the flag is still refused client-side, and the refusal now names `--doc-types <docType,...|all>`.
- Post-bind guidance for a scoped slot lists `bind`, `bind-status ... backfilling`, `rag:reembed -- --profile <slot> --tenant <key>`, `record-binding-evidence` and `activate-binding`.
- `ft-consent revoke` prints every fine-tune binding the taint moved from active or backfilling to available.
- `rag_finetune_clear_slot` now refuses while a binding of the slot is serving. Its final call deletes the slot's binding rows and resets doc_types.
- Switches: no new variable. With RAG_FINETUNE off nothing changes. The 065x parity test (06.1-17) passed with 0654 present, since its regex leaves out 0654 too.
