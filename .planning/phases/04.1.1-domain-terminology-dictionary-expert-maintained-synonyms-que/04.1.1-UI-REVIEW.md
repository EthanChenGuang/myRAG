## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Row actions off-screen at 375 px** — on mobile only Term and Variants are visible; Edit/Delete/Hide need a horizontal scroll the user may not discover — move Actions to the first column below `md`, or render rows as stacked cards below `md`.
2. **Off-scale spacing `gap-3` / `mt-3`** — drifts from the 03.2 scale the contract reuses — replace with `gap-2` or `gap-4` and `mt-2`/`mt-4`.
3. **Duplicate failure announcement** — the 422 summary appears as a focused alert and again in the status line — leave the status line empty (or neutral) when the role=alert summary is rendered.
