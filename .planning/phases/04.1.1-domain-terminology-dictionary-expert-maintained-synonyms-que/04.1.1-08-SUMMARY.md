# Phase 4.1.1 Plan 08: Miss log and mining storage Summary

**Migration 0473 adds the tenant-scoped miss log and candidate table with seven service-role-only functions. Rows expire after 30 days without any schedule. With `RAG_TERMINOLOGY_MINING=true`, the v2 finish path records each missed chat, embed or voice request. The query is passed through `maskPII`, cut to 500 characters, and written fire-and-forget. With the switch off (the default), nothing changes.**

## Threats

- T-0411-21 (PII in the miss log): opt-in switch, off by default. Queries go through `maskPII` and are cut to 500 characters. Misses are deleted after 30 days, on every record call and through `purge_misses`. Candidate examples older than 30 days are cleared. Access is service role only. The failure log carries only a code.
- T-0411-22 (cross-tenant df): df filters on `tenant_key` and current, ready chunks. A two-tenant test shows that tenant B's identical chunk is not counted for A, and that superseded and processing chunks are excluded.
- T-0411-23 (latency): the hook is `void recordMiss(...)`. An integration test shows a hanging write does not delay `runRetrieval`. The write is aborted at 500 ms, and a client that ignores the abort is cut by the race.
