### Residual notes (not counted)

- The `terminology-rpc` boundary rule matches only literal `.rpc('rag_term_…')`/`.from('rag_term_…')`; `src/lib/rag/feedback-loop/terminology-bridge.ts` (Phase 5.1.1) calls `rag_term_record_miss` through a constant.
