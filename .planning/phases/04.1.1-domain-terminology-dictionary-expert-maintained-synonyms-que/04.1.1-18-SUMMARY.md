# Phase 4.1.1 Plan 18: Bounded rerank-input reservation for expansion-only hits Summary

**After fusion and dedupe, up to `TerminologyParams.reserve_slots` (default 2) of the best-ranked `term:0` hits that fell outside the rerank input replace its lowest-ranked entries. They never take more than half of the input. They keep their fused score and stand last, so expansion now reaches the reranker at every cap the contract allows, even with full original lists. With the terminology switch off, with no expansion tokens, or with `reserve_slots` 0, the output is byte-identical to before.**

## Accomplishments

- `reserveTerminologyHits` (pure, in `src/lib/rag/terminology/reserve.ts`) applies the reservation. It uses the same parent-aware rule as `dedupeAndCap` to decide what is already in the rerank input.
- The search stage calls it inside the `dedupe` stage function, so the stage's recorded counts carry `term_reserved`, and also on the `sliceCap` fallback.
- `DedupeCounts.term_reserved` is optional, so serialized counts are unchanged when the reservation does not run.
- The query stage's `TerminologyOutcome` carries `reserveSlots`, and the pipeline passes it to the search stage.
- No pool, cap, leg weight or `rrf_k` literal was added to terminology code. Tests pass `rrf_k` and the pool as explicit values and read the cap range and pool maximum from the generated contract.

## Deviations

- **The cap range comes from the contract, not the run-time guard.** The generated `RetrievalParamsSchema` accepts `rerank_input_cap` 25..100 (Phase 4.2's floor is already in the contract). `guardCap` in `src/lib/rag/v2/retrieval-params.ts` still rejects overrides below 50 and keeps the previous value, with the warning "rerank_input_cap must be 50..100". So an eval override of 25 resolves to 75 today. The reach test therefore builds its params through `RetrievalParamsSchema.parse({...effectiveRetrievalParams(...), rerank_input_cap: cap})`. That keeps the test on the contract's range, as the plan requires. `retrieval-params.ts` is not in this plan's files, and lowering the guard is Phase 4.2's job (LAT-01, RNK-02), so I did not change it. **Phase 4.2 should align `guardCap` with the contract.**
- `src/lib/rag/v2/stages/query.ts` and `src/lib/rag/v2/pipeline.ts` were also edited, though the plan does not list them. This was the minimal change needed to carry the terminology params in force for the request (`reserve_slots`) from the stage that reads them to the search stage.
