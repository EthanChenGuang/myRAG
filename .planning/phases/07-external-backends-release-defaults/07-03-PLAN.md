Build the two pure pieces every external tenant check rests on: the lower-case tenant-key helper (A-5) and the Qdrant scoped-request wrapper with its assertion (D-08, A-3). Isolation layer 1 for Qdrant is tested here on its own.

| app to Qdrant | a shared collection holds every tenant; only the request filter separates them |
| scope to key | case-sensitive vendor matching versus case-insensitive UUID validation |

| T-07-08 | Information disclosure | lookup_from or shard_key selecting another collection or shard | high | mitigate | rejected at any depth |
