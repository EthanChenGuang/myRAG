<task type="checkpoint:decision" gate="blocking-human">
  <name>Task 1: Vercel plan and the drain scheduler (D-04 b, A-6)</name>
  <decision>Which scheduler drives POST or GET /api/internal/vector-sync/drain in production</decision>
  <context>The drain route, the after() kick and the CLI exist (plan 07-13); the default lag bound is RAG_VECTOR_SYNC_MAX_LAG_SECONDS=300, and a tenant whose outbox lag exceeds it is not ready and falls back to pgvector. Vercel Cron cannot run minutely on Hobby, and a minutely crons entry fails Hobby deployments (research Pitfall 8). pg_net calls are asynchronous; the recipe uses timeout_milliseconds 58000 (Pitfall 9). The choice is reversible by switching schedulers.</context>
  <options>
    <option id="pg-cron">
      <name>Supabase pg_cron plus pg_net plus Vault (recommended)</name>
      <pros>Works on any Vercel plan; minutely; secrets stay in Vault; recipe already shipped in supabase/optional/vector-sync-schedule.sql</pros>
      <cons>Needs the pg_cron and pg_net extensions enabled on the Supabase project; cron.job_run_details needs the shipped cleanup job</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="vercel-cron-pro">
      <name>Vercel Cron on Pro</name>
      <pros>No database extensions; minutely on Pro</pros>
      <cons>Best-effort delivery with possible duplicates (safe because of leases); needs CRON_SECRET equal to RAG_VECTOR_SYNC_SECRET and a crons entry in vercel.json that would fail if the project ever moves to Hobby</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="vercel-schedules-hobby">
      <name>Vercel Schedules (beta) on Hobby</name>
      <pros>No extensions and no plan upgrade</pros>
      <cons>15-minute cadence with jitter and a monthly run cap; with the 300 s lag bound tenants are not ready between runs unless after() kicks keep up, so RAG_VECTOR_SYNC_MAX_LAG_SECONDS must be raised to at least 1200 or the backend stays degraded; beta limits may change</cons>
    </option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select: pg-cron, vercel-cron-pro or vercel-schedules-hobby, and state the Vercel plan</resume-signal>
  <reversibility rating="reversible">Switching schedulers needs no migration; the route serves all three.</reversibility>
</task>
