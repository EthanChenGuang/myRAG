## Behaviour changes (all behind RAG_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS, default off)

- `auto` reads the per-mode cell of `eval/config/output-caps.json`; both cells are committed off, so `auto` caps nothing until the 05.2-26 sweep writes a cell.

- Excluded, limit unchanged and no concise part: request with its own `maxOutputTokens`, reasoning, flexible or bypass, no knowledge-base context, approval continuation, voice.
- WhatsApp keeps its limit and gets only `[rule:concise]`.
- Claude Citations path: `min(16384, cap)`.
