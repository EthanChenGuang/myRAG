<objective>
Add credential and account-request handling to the output guard: the rules as data on the shared matcher, sentence buffering, the block-or-warn decision, late blocks with recorded ranges, and the scan that keeps every registry text free of guard findings.

Purpose: SEC-06 (credential half), D-39; D-63 step 8. The stream adapter and notice wiring are plan 05.2-13.
Output: rules module, rule data, exported normaliser, guard extension, fixture additions, tests.

Decisions recorded here (Claude's discretion within D-39): rule phrases are security vocabulary only (words for passwords, verification codes, log-in, account confirmation) paired with request forms (imperatives and questions asking the reader to give, enter, send or confirm); the guard reports warnings and blocks through its summary and an `onNotice` callback, because it does not emit notices itself.

Depends on 05.2-11 (guard core, rules file, fixture file), 05.2-04 (registry for the text scan) and 05.1-01 (last editor of the shared injection-rule matcher).

Anchors: grep `normalise` and `scanText` in src/lib/rag/security/injection-scan.ts and `injectionRulesVersion` in injection-rules.ts; re-read the newest definitions.
Commit rule for every task: plain `git commit`; the message ends with the attribution lines the executor's own session supplies; never copy them from a plan.
</objective>
