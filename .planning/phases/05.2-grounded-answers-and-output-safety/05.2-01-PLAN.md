<objective>
Define every new answer-side and safety contract of Phase 5.2 as Pydantic v2 models with generated Zod, and extend the existing answer, citation, groundedness and review contracts (D-59).

Purpose: D-63 step 1. Every later plan imports these shapes; nothing hand-writes a TypeScript twin. This plan also fixes the backend fields the UI-SPEC asks for in section 8.1 (text part index and hash on sentence records, blocked ranges, `textsSource`).
Output: four new contract modules, three modified ones, registration, samples, regenerated schemas and Zod, one pytest file.

Contract decisions recorded here (Claude's discretion within D-23 and D-59):
- Field names inside `checks` follow D-23 literally (snake_case: `text_hash`, `output_guard`, `version_conflict`, `finish_reason`); AnswerRecord and CitationRecord keep their existing camelCase.
- `AnswerSentence.part` is the index of the message text part whose text the offsets address; `text_hash` is the lowercase hex SHA-256 of that part's text as persisted (UI-SPEC 8.1 backend rows). Offsets are UTF-16 code units (RESEARCH Pitfall 8).
- `AnswerChecks` reserves no 5.2.1 field; Phase 5.2.1 adds optional `calc` and `prompt_budget` in its own contract version (D-23, listed in the PLAN-INDEX under "Provides to 5.2.1").
- `answer_language` keeps one meaning per place (D-89): the question's language travels on the trace and the record column; the language detected from the answer text is stored as `checks.text_language`.

Depends on 03.2-06 (last Phase 3.2 contract plan before this one regenerates the same files) and 05.1.1-22 (Phase 5.1.1 closed; its contract plans also regenerate these files). Rebase and regenerate; never hand-merge a generated file.
