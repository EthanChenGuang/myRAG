# Phase 5.2 Plan 12: Credential and account-request handling in the output guard Summary

**The output guard now recognizes sentences that ask the reader for a password, code or sign-in details with data rules on the shared NFKC matcher, blocks them when the answer removed a link or the turn had flagged context, warns otherwise, and records late blocks that the whole-text function applies exactly.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 75 min
- **Tasks:** 3, **commits:** 6 (2 RED, 2 GREEN, 1 gate test, this summary)

## Accomplishments

- `credential-rules.ts` loads `config/rag/output-guard-rules.json` through the generated `OutputGuardRulesSchema`, rejects obfuscation patterns with back-references (`\1`, `\k<x>`) and duplicate ids, and matches sentences with `normalise` from `injection-scan.ts`.
- `injection-scan.ts` exports `normalise` and `hasBackreference` with unchanged bodies; the injection-scan, rules-clean-corpus, ingest and tool-result tests pass unchanged.
- The guard's credential stage sits after the link stage. Ordinary prose still streams with at most a word held (tested at 16 characters); a credential sentence is held at most 600 characters.
- 12 synthetic credential fixture entries on reserved hosts; the split-point property test (every split, every pair under 120 characters, enforce and flag) passes for all 55 entries.
- Registry scan: 115 texts (notices, labels, instructions of zh, en, it) pass `guardWholeText` in enforce with an empty allowlist, with and without flagged context, and match no credential rule. A mutation that adds "asked for" as a request term makes the scan fail on the English blocked and warning notices. **No registry text was reworded.**
