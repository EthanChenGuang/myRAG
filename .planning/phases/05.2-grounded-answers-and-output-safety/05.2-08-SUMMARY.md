# Phase 5.2 Plan 08: No-answer sentinel Summary

**The model's "nothing in the context answers this" signal is now a fixed token. A stream adapter recognizes it, whole or split, at the start of any step and replaces it with the server's refusal text. Planted copies of the token in retrieved documents are made inert while a rule switch is on.**

## Accomplishments

- `src/lib/rag/generation/sentinel.ts` holds the token, the pure matcher, `applySentinelToText`, `sentinelTransform` and `neutralizeSentinel`. 05.2-09 installs the adapter after the citation transform and passes `notices.no_information` (or `notices.no_information_web` when webSearch was offered).
- Stream behaviour: ordinary answers are released as soon as they diverge from the token. A step whose first text block is the token emits the refusal and reports `onRefusal({ refusalOrigin: 'model_relevance' })`. Earlier step text is kept and the refusal follows it as a new paragraph. A held prefix at a `length` finish is released as it stands, and no test needs a timeout.
- `buildContext` (legacy, v2 and references all go through it): with `RAG_CONTEXT_RELEVANCE_RULE` or `RAG_PARTIAL_ANSWER_RULE` on, the token cannot reach the model through the context string, the block texts or the block titles, and the count is returned as `neutralizedSentinels`. With both off, the output is deep-equal to before.
- `tests/helpers/stream-model.ts` is the shared real-`streamText` helper for the guard, cut-off and notice stream tests.
