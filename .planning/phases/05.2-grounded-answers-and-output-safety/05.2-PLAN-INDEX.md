## Planner resolutions (Claude's discretion, recorded in the plans)

- The no-answer sentinel is a fixed ASCII token; a match at the start of a step is always a refusal, with or without trailing text (plan 08; D-16, D-93).
- The chat route reads an optional body field `channel` (`web` | `whatsapp`) for notice rendering, so plan 09 does not wait for the WhatsApp plan.
