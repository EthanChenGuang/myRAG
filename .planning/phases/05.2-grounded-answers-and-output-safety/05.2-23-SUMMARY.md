# 05.2-23 Summary: sidecar claims endpoint

**The sidecar now scores every factual sentence of an answer in one authenticated request: claims are routed by language, premises are built per claim from the refs, scorers work in batches, and a deadline returns partial results where unfinished claims are `not_checked`/`timeout`, never unsupported.**

## Deviations

- `score_many` takes an additional optional keyword `on_result`. Without it, a deadline that cancels a running batch would lose the scores already finished. The planned signature `score_many(pairs) -> list[float | None]` still works.
- The plan says the `it` language goes to neither scorer and is `unsupported_language`. That holds. Additionally, `zh-hans` without `allow_vectara` or a Vectara key is `unsupported_language` (the plan truth: "otherwise every claim is not_checked with reason unsupported_language"), and `en` with no usable local scorer is `error`. The contract enum has no `scorer_unavailable`.
- No migration, no new environment variable, no new package.
