## Deviations from Plan

1. **`credential_rules` is an empty list, not an empty map per language.** The 05.2-01 contract defines `credential_rules` as a list of `CredentialRule` with a `language` field; a map would not validate. 05.2-12 adds entries to the list.
2. **`special_use` key in the TLD snapshot.** The plan names `{ version, tlds }`; I added `source` and `special_use` (RFC 6761/6762/7686/9476 names). Without them the reserved fixture hosts (`*.test`, `*.example`) would not be bare domains and `other.test` in prose would pass. `isPublicTld` reads `tlds` only.
3. **Hold for prose is one word plus a short lookahead**, not strictly one word: a word followed by a space waits for up to about ten characters of the next word (ending early when that word is longer than four letters or not a label) to rule out a spelled-out separator. The test asserts the observed release.
4. **Extra exports** beyond the artifact list (`createOutputLinkPolicy`, `decideLink`, `scanAt`, `compileOutputGuardRules`, `defaultOutputGuardRules`, `isUrlBody`, `isTerminator`, bounds constants, `flushAll`) for 05.2-12/13 to build on without re-implementing.
