Decisions recorded here (Claude's discretion, D-02 and RESEARCH Pattern 11):
- Tenant key form, used for all three new tables: a copied `tenant_key text NOT NULL` validated by `public.rag_assert_tenant_key` inside every writer and reader function, so 0942 and 0943 are common files with no mode variants. Document ownership checks compare `documents.tenant_key`, a generated column in both modes since 0301.
- Window of an alert: `[window_end - RAG_UPLOADER_ALERT_WINDOW_HOURS, window_end)` where `window_end` is the hour boundary after the event; the unique key `(tenant_key, uploader_id, window_start)` makes the alert fire once per window.
- No new function runs with definer rights; all are `SECURITY INVOKER` with `SET search_path = ''` and schema-qualified names, executed by service_role (the 0603 pattern).
