# Phase 4.4 Plan 12: BM25 lexical benchmark Summary

**`scripts/rag/bench-lexical-bm25.mts` measures the `ts_rank_cd` leg of `hybrid_search_chunks` against `rag_bm25_search_chunks` on a neutral Zipf tenant. For each query shape it reports timings, the plan of the statement each function executes, and how often the 500-candidate cap changes the result. The 30,000-chunk PGlite report is committed.**

## Command

```
node --import tsx scripts/rag/bench-lexical-bm25.mts --chunks 30000 --queries 12 --seed 44 --out eval/reports/phase-04.4
```

- Flags: `--chunks <n>[,<n>...]` (one table per size), `--queries`, `--seed`, `--out`, `--database-url-env <NAME>`, `--runs` (15), `--warmup` (3), `--limit` (75), `--candidate-limit` (500), `--vocabulary` (20000), `--min-length` (80), `--max-length` (400).
- Exit codes: 0 done; 2 for a usage error, an unset variable, a URL given as an argument, a single-tenant schema or a missing `pg`; 1 for a failure or an incomplete cleanup.
- Corpus size run: **30,000 chunks**. The run took 4 min 19 s, so the 10k fallback was not needed.

## Results (PGlite, 30,000 chunks, 3 queries per shape, 15 measured runs)

| shape | matched rows (median) | candidates | ts_rank_cd p50 / p95 ms | BM25 p50 / p95 ms | BM25 / ts_rank_cd p50 | uncapped BM25 ms | overlap@10 mean (min) | overlap@50 mean (min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rare_single | 126 | 126 | 7.97 / 9.96 | 29.45 / 35.28 | 3.7 | 27 | 1 (1) | 1 (1) |
| mixed_rare_common | 27,756 | 500 | 469 / 594 | 713 / 891 | 1.52 | 6,028 | 1 (1) | 1 (1) |
| many_terms | 6,050 | 500 | 268 / 327 | 452 / 566 | 1.68 | 1,705 | 1 (1) | 1 (1) |
| common_only | 29,827 | 500 | 406 / 511 | 577 / 769 | 1.42 | 5,910 | 0.5 (0.3) | 0.5 (0.36) |

- **Ratios.** BM25 costs 1.4 to 1.7 times the `ts_rank_cd` leg on broad queries. It is about 3.7 times on the rare single term, where the absolute cost is small (about 22 ms more). Exact uncapped BM25 is 4 to 13 times slower than the capped two-pass version (research R-3 confirmed).
- **Overlap.** The cap leaves the result unchanged whenever any query term discriminates. For common-only queries, overlap@50 is 0.36 to 0.67 (research R-4). The mean overlap@50 over all 12 queries is 0.875, below the D-24 fixture gate of 0.95. That gate belongs to plans 04.4-13 and 14, which measure the fixture eval set; this synthetic mix deliberately includes the worst shape. The real tokenizer drops stopwords, which removes most common-only queries. The live run of 04.4-15 measures the real rate.
- **Plans.** Rare terms use a bitmap scan on `idx_document_chunks_lexical_tsv`. Queries that match 20% to 99% of the corpus fall back to a sequential scan on `document_chunks`. D-27 asks for these matched-set sizes for its document-frequency pruning decision; at 1M chunks, the queries that match nearly every chunk are the ones pruning would bound.
- PGlite timings are **not** the budget measurement (`search` 400 ms chat and embed, 300 ms voice). The report says so and names the real-Postgres run of plan 04.4-15 as that measurement.

## Real-Postgres route (for plan 04.4-15)

```
EVAL_DB_URL=... node --import tsx scripts/rag/bench-lexical-bm25.mts --chunks 30000,300000,1000000 --queries 12 --seed 44 \
  --database-url-env EVAL_DB_URL --out eval/reports/phase-04.4-live
```

- The URL is read only from the named variable. It is never printed, and in real mode an error prints only its SQLSTATE or class.
- The tool connects through the `pg` client of the locked tree and refuses a single-tenant schema. It creates a throwaway `auth.users` row and tenant `u:<random uuid>`, runs `ANALYZE` on the two tables, enables and rebuilds statistics, and measures.
- When a size finishes, it deletes the chunks and documents, calls `rag_lexstats_disable` and deletes the user. It then records the rows left in `document_chunks`, `documents`, `rag_lexical_stats_state`, `rag_lexical_stats_pending`, `rag_lexical_df`, `rag_lexical_corpus` and `auth.users`; any non-zero count makes the exit code 1.
- Plans are taken with `LOAD 'auto_explain'`. If the role may not load it, the tool writes `explain-statements-<size>.sql` for the operator and the report says so.
- Output files: `benchmark-postgres.md` and `benchmark-postgres.json`. The session sets `statement_timeout` to 300 s.

3. **Extra flags** (`--runs`, `--warmup`, `--limit`, `--candidate-limit`, corpus shape) so the test runs fast and the live step can vary the cap.
