## Why the three feature gates read zero: the v2 refusal floor in ingest mode (pre-existing)

In every `RAG_PIPELINE=v2` ingest run, most items are refused before assembly with `below_similarity_floor`. This includes
`tpl-v2-off-ingest`, where every Phase 3.3 switch is off, so this plan layers and seeds nothing:

| Run | refused (below_similarity_floor) | answered | overall Recall@10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| tpl-all-off-ingest (legacy pipeline) | 124 (`no_relevant_context`) | 376 | 0.710 |
| tpl-v2-off-prechunked | 21 | 335 | 0.901 |
| tpl-v2-off-ingest | 423 | 77 | 0.168 |
| tpl-v2-off-ingest on base commit d0196d19 (before this plan) | 423 | 77 | 0.168 |

Of the 78 answerable templated items, 75 to 78 are refused in every v2 ingest run. Example: `ingest-tpl-quantity-en-01` in
`tpl-recommended-ingest` has the gold chunk ("Pluplix of the jekgrork is 47.38 kHz.", tpl-family-en-01.pdf) as its first candidate, and
the refusal stage drops it. Recall, MRR and the templated rates are scored after refusal, so they read 0.

So the zero rates measure the v2 similarity floor applied to the fixture embedder in ingest mode (`loadFixtureScoreCalibration('ingest')`
and the ingest profile floor), not the Phase 3.3 features. Before the official comparison, plan 03.3-25 needs one of:
- a calibrated ingest-mode floor for v2 (`python -m rag_eval.calibrate refusal`, which also covers the new quantity lexical versions 6
  to 9: see the `[RAG Relex]` reminder of plan 03.3-21), or
- a decision that the templated gates are read on candidates before refusal.

Neither the gates file nor a threshold was edited here.
