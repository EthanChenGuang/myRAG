# 03.3-23: declared fragment longer than the shipped fragment cap

Date: 2026-11-27. Found while building the seeded fixture (plan 03.3-23, Task 2). For plan 03.3-25 to decide; nothing was changed.

## Observation

With `tpl-recommended` (`RAG_PAGE_BOILERPLATE=both`), the restricted ingest build (one family member per language plus the three
variant files) removes every declared top and bottom line from the stored chunks. The declared fragment is removed everywhere except
in `tpl-variants-en` and `tpl-variants-it`, where 4 chunks each still hold it. The family files lose the fragment through detect mode
(it sits at a page edge there); in the variant files it sits mid-page, so only the fragment rule can remove it.

## Cause

`cleanBoilerplate` (src/lib/rag/ingest/prepare/boilerplate.ts) applies `min(rule.max_length, config.fragment_max_length)`.
The fixture rule (`eval/fixtures/ingest/templated/boilerplate-rules.json`) has `max_length: 24`; the shipped
`config/rag/boilerplate-rules.json` has `fragment_max_length: 12`. The zh fragment is within 12 characters; the en and it fragments
are 14 characters, so the effective cap of 12 keeps them.

## Effect

The fixture `boilerplate_token_share` of the en and it variant files includes these fragment tokens. The fixture test
(`tests/rag-db/fixture-phase33-features.test.mts`) asserts fragment removal only where the fragment fits the effective cap.

## Options (not taken here)

- Shorten the en and it fixture fragments to 12 characters or fewer (fixture data of plan 03.3-20; changes the corpus hash).
- Raise `fragment_max_length` in the shipped config (a product default; needs the ship rule).
- Accept: a tenant rule cannot widen the deployment cap, which is the intended guard.
