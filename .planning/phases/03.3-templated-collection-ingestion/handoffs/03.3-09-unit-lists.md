# 03.3-09 hand-off: two unit lists (D-32)

Date: 2026-11-26. For: the Phase 3.3 coordinator.

After plan 03.3-09 the repository holds two independent lists of measurement units:

1. `config/rag/language-packs.json` (and its byte-identical copy `rag-sidecar/config/rag/language-packs.json`),
   top-level block `quantity` (`QuantityNotation`: `unit_symbols`, `decimal_separators`, `grouping_separators`).
   Read by `src/lib/rag/lexical/tokenize.ts` through `parseLanguageRegistry`; used only while `RAG_LEXICAL_QUANTITY`
   is on (callers wired by plan 03.3-21). General SI/data symbols and `%` only.
2. `src/lib/rag/query/rewrite-gate.lexicon.json`, `patterns.number_with_unit` (a regular expression with currency
   words and a few units), read by `src/lib/rag/query/protected.ts` to protect quantities from query rewriting.

They are not unified in Phase 3.3 (D-32). A later plan that unifies them must keep the rewrite gate's currency words
out of the lexical registry block (they are not general measurement symbols) and must not change token output while
the quantity switch is off.
