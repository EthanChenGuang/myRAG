## Fixtures

- Ids: `tpl-family-<lang>-01` to `-12` and `tpl-variants-<lang>` for zh, en and it (39). All `product_technical`, digital, 4 pages, source `config_upload`, source key `root/<id>.pdf`. They are recorded with hashes 4001–4039 into `templated_fixtures`. `fixtures`, `long_table_fixtures` and every earlier PDF and recording are byte-identical.
- Family: five level-2 headings `1.`–`5.` (shared); 32 Markdown lines, of which 4 differ between any two members (identity paragraph on page 1, one member sentence in section 2, one in section 4, the quantity sentence in section 3), so 87.5% of lines are equal.
- Quantity: one per member. Per language there are at least 5 spelling styles (decimal point or comma, with or without a space, unit in mixed, upper or lower case, apostrophe or comma digit grouping). Units are `kHz` (en), `kWh` (it) and `mAh` (zh), all in the registry block. Member 7 (en) and member 8 (zh, it) are the marked members with one separator followed by exactly three digits.
- Variant file: a contents run of the five heading lines on page 1, then pages 2–4 each restart at `1. <word> <designation>`. The designations are `<Stem>-11..13` (en), `-21..23` (it) and `-31..33` (zh). The first body line repeats the designation, and there is one designation-specific sentence per section.
- Declared lines, per document and per language: on each of the 4 pages, the top line is the first body block, the bottom line is the last body block, one fragment (≤ 2 tokens) stands between two paragraphs, and one footer sits in the furniture layer. That makes 4 top, 4 bottom, 4 fragment and 4 footer lines. Top, bottom and footer differ across pages only in the page number. The recordings keep top, bottom and fragment in Markdown and chunks and drop the footer (`page_footer`, `furniture`). The declared lines are at least 5% of the cl100k tokens of each Markdown.
