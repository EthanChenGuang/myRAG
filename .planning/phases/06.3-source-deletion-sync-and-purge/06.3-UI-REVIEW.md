# Phase 06.3 — UI Review

**Audited:** 2026-11-28
**Baseline:** 06.3-UI-SPEC.md (approved 2026-11-25)
**Screenshots:** captured (Config UI only; 89 PNGs in `.planning/ui-reviews/06.3-20261128-075430/`, gitignored)
**Interaction captures:** off (`workflow.ui_interaction_capture` is false). Interactions were driven with Playwright clicks instead: purge dialog, purge result, the Settings sub-tab, the Remove documents dialog and the SharePoint picker.

## Rendering method

- The real `scripts/config-ui/index.html` was served with `python3 -m http.server 8781` and rendered in Playwright Chromium at `deviceScaleFactor: 1`, viewport height 900.
- Every `**/api/**` request was mocked. The bodies use synthetic ids and the fixture shapes from `tests/ui/config-source-sync-panel.test.ts` and `config-purge-panel.test.ts`.
- **Capture matrix:** 3 locales (en-US, zh-CN, it-IT) × 3 widths (360, 768, 1024). For each cell: Source sync with Connections populated (in_step, paused+overdue, not_run, failed), the Settings sub-tab, Purge records populated (verified, pending, remaining, unchecked) with all disclosures open, the purge dialog, the purge result, the Connectors provider list and the SharePoint picker at the Sites level. The SharePoint settings card was captured at 360 and 1024 only.
- **Extra states** (en and zh, 1024): empty, switched off (404), error (500) and sign-in (401) for both panels, plus the focus check and the Remove documents dialog.
- **Programmatic checks:** horizontal overflow per panel, elements past the viewport, controls under 24px, which element has focus when a dialog opens, the computed focus outline, and English text left inside zh/it panels.
- **Not rendered:**
  - The Purge mode dialog. Selecting Purge on the first row did not open a dialog because that row was already in Purge mode, so this was not captured.
  - The app surfaces (ConnectorGrid pill and withdrawn count, withdrawn citations). They are Next.js and need a running app with auth. They were reviewed from code and tests only.

## Pillar Scores

| Pillar | Score | Key Finding |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| 1. Copywriting | 3/4 | Spec copy is followed closely in all 3 locales; the switched-off/404 state shows the bare "Request failed (404)." |
| 2. Visuals | 2/4 | At 360px the Status column, the mode select and the Check column (the declared anchors) are off-screen inside horizontal scroll; the paused block is clipped |
| 3. Color | 2/4 | The paused-run buttons render white text on the amber `alert-warning` (about 1.6:1); white "Reload panel" on the red alert is under 4.5:1 |
| 4. Typography | 3/4 | 4 sizes / 2 weights respected, tabular right-aligned receipt; the purge dialog title renders dimmer than its body text |

| 6. Experience Design | 3/4 | All states of 7.1 present, focus lands on the safe button; the select focus ring is near-invisible (2px at 20% alpha) |

**Overall: 16/24**

## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Paused-run buttons are unreadable** (HIGH): white text on amber. The admin cannot read the destructive action. Give the buttons a solid `bg-base-100` background, or use `btn-neutral` / text-base-content inside the alert.
2. **360px loses the anchor columns** (HIGH): the Status, Mode and Check columns sit in horizontal scroll, and the paused block is clipped mid-sentence. Below 768px, stack Status and Mode under the Connection cell (as the document count already is) and show Check in the detail row. Put the paused block outside the scrolling table (a full-width row with `sticky left-0` and the viewport width), or as a block after the table.
3. **The 404 switched-off state is an error, not an explanation** (MED). Map a list 404 to a neutral "This feature is switched off in this deployment" alert (plain `alert`, not `alert-error`).

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (3/4)
- **MED** `source-sync.js` `mapSourceSyncError` (~l.470) and the purge.js equivalent: a list 404 (switched off or route missing) renders `请求失败（404）。` / "Request failed (404)." in an `alert-error`. Evidence: `sourcesync-off-zh-1024.png`, `purgerecords-off-en-1024.png`. Fix: add an `error.off` sentence in en/zh/it.
- **LOW** `pickers/sharepoint-scope-picker.js:592` and the picker strings ("Show selection", "Included by …", "Open site") are English under zh/it (`picker-zh-360.png`). The spec treats the Connectors tab as English-only, so this is consistent. It is still noted, because the picker sits next to localized panels.
- **LOW** In the zh/it Purge records descriptions, "Document Processing" and "Purge" stay in English (`purgerecords-zh-360.png`). This is acceptable because they name English UI labels, but they are not wrapped in `lang="en"` (spec 5.1 rule).
- Pass: every CTA verb chain matches section 1: "Purge document", "Keep document", "Remove documents…", "Dismiss run", "Check again", "Refresh records".

### Pillar 2: Visuals (2/4)
- **HIGH** `source-sync.js:799` table region: at 360 the region scrolls (683/231px in it, 550/231 in en, 427/231 in zh). Status badges and the deletion-mode select (the only row control, and the spec 6.10 anchor) are off-screen. The paused block's colspan cell is wider than the viewport, so its body text, the "Can be confirmed until" time and "Dismiss run" are cut off (`sourcesync-it-360.png`). The it locale also overflows at 768 (823/622).
- **HIGH** Purge records at 360: the Check badge (the 6.10 anchor) and "Check again" are past the right edge. Only When and Started by are visible (`purgerecords-zh-360.png`; region 328/231).
- **MED** `sharepoint-scope-picker.js:588`: names `truncate` to 76px at 360, so "Site B w…" is unreadable. The full name is only in `title`, which touch users cannot reach (`picker-en-360.png`). Fix: allow 2-line wrapping (`line-clamp-2 break-words`) or drop the button label to the icon plus an aria-label below `sm`.
- Pass at 1024: a clear hierarchy, badges are the only colour in the table, and the receipt's Left column reads as a column of zeros with ⚠ on non-zero values (`purgerecords-en-1024.png`).

### Pillar 3: Color (2/4)
- **HIGH** `source-sync.js:1081`: `btn btn-error btn-outline` and `btn btn-outline` inside `alert-warning` render white text and borders on amber, roughly 1.6:1 (`sourcesync-en-1024.png`, `sourcesync-it-360.png`). This fails the spec 1 quality floor (4.5:1) on the destructive control.
- **MED** `source-sync.js:743` (and purge.js): "Reload panel" is a white outline button on the `alert-error` red, about 2.8:1 (`purgerecords-error-en-1024.png`). Fix: `btn-sm bg-base-100 text-base-content`.
- Pass: accent is not overused, and status is never shown by colour alone (icon plus word on every badge).

### Pillar 4: Typography (3/4)
- **LOW** In the purge dialog the title (`text-base font-semibold`) renders in a muted grey, lighter than the 14px body (`purgedialog-it-360.png`). The hierarchy inverts. Fix: drop any `/70` opacity class from the title, or set `text-base-content`.
- **LOW** Unknown store ids render in `font-mono text-xs` per spec, but they look out of place in the receipt next to the 14px labels. This is acceptable by contract.
- Pass: the `tabular-nums` right-aligned Removed/Left columns, mono workspace keys with `break-all`, and 4 sizes and 2 weights.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)
- **MED** Touch targets at 360: `details > summary` "Show files" / "Show check" are 20px tall, and the picker checkboxes are 20×20 (measure.jsonl). The spec asks for 48px row actions below 768px. Fix: `summary.py-2 min-h-12` or `md:min-h-0`, and `checkbox` → `checkbox-md` below md.
- **LOW** The picker "Remove" button is `btn-xs` (23px tall).
- Pass: no arbitrary values were seen; toolbar `gap-2 mb-4`, `p-2` picker rows and `btn md:btn-sm` row actions match the scale.

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)
- **MED** Focus on the deletion-mode select: the computed outline is `2px oklch(… / 0.2)`, essentially invisible on base-200 (`focus-select-en-1024.png`). Buttons get a solid 3px ring. Fix: `focus-visible:outline-primary` / `focus:outline-offset-2` on the selects and on the purge reason select.
- **LOW** With the purge dialog open at 360, the document is 478px wide (docOverflow=true). The cause is the underlying Documents table, not the dialog, but it lets the page scroll sideways behind the modal.
- Pass:
  - Purge dialog focus lands on "Keep document" in all 9 cells.
  - Confirm stays disabled with "Choose a reason to continue."
  - The result view replaces the content with the receipt plus "Open purge records" and "Close dialog".
  - Empty, sign-in and 500 states render with Reload actions.
  - The status region announces counts.
  - No page errors were logged across 9 contexts.
- **Not verified (code only):** the app ConnectorGrid pill/note and `syncCountParts` withdrawn count (covered by `tests/ui/connector-sync-withdrawn.test.ts` and `connector-reconcile-warning.test.ts`); withdrawn citation frames.
