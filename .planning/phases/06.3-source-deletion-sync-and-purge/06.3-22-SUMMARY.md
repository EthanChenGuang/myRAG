# 06.3-22 Summary: purge in the Config UI and the withdrawn sync count

Admins can now purge a document from the Documents table. The dialog needs a reason and sends only `reason_code` and `reason_note`. After the purge the dialog shows the receipt, the store-by-store table whose Left column is the column of zeros. The new Purge records panel lists every purge with its check, and Check again runs the check once more. The app connector card also names files that a sync skipped because an admin purged them (`SyncStats.withdrawn`, left to this plan by 06.3-21).

## Behaviour changes

- **Documents table.** A `Purge` button (`btn-error btn-outline`, `bi-file-earmark-x`) follows Delete. It is built with DOM calls and appears only when `window.openPurgeDialog` exists. Its `aria-label` is "Purge {filename}: remove it from every store and from past citations". The click passes the raw id, file name and source. The existing three buttons are unchanged. The Document Management heading gets `id="documents-management-title"` and `tabindex="-1"`, so focus can return there.
- **Purge dialog** (native `dialog.modal`, localized en/zh/it):
  - Focus starts on Keep document. Purge document stays disabled until a reason is chosen and, for Other, the note has a non-space character. A `role="status"` line says what is missing.
  - The note is capped at 500 characters.
  - While the request runs, every control is disabled and Escape and the backdrop do nothing.
  - On success the dialog stays open and shows the focused result title, the body for pending or remaining (with the record id in monospace) and the receipt, plus Open purge records and Close dialog. Closing reloads the Documents table and focuses its heading.
  - Refusals: 404 shows `error.notFound` and leaves only Close dialog. 400 shows `error.invalidReason` and sets `aria-invalid` on the select. 403 `read_only` shows `error.readOnly`, and 401/403 show the sign-in sentence. 5xx or a network failure shows `error.failed`. In every case the values are kept.
- **Receipt.** A `table.table-sm.w-auto` with a caption, row headers, and Removed and Left columns right-aligned in `tabular-nums`, in the server's store order. A non-zero Left value is bold, with a warning icon and hidden text. A missing count shows an em dash with "Not reported". Unknown store ids (such as `external:<target>`) are shown in monospace. It shows the pending-files and queued-external lines, the check time and the not-covered note. It shows counts only, never names, paths or hashes.
- **Purge records panel** (`#rag-administration-purge`, placed after Source sync):
  - The toolbar has Open Document Processing, the updated time and Refresh records.
  - The table shows two rows per record: When, Started by (admin id shortened, with the full id in `title`, or "Source sync, {provider}", plus the workspace key), Reason with its note (hidden below 768px), Document id and version count (hidden below 1024px), and the Check badge.
  - The detail row has Show check (the receipt or `result.noCheck`) and Check again, which posts to `admin/purges/{id}/verify`, replaces the badge and the receipt, opens the disclosure and announces the result. A 404 on Check again shows `error.recordGone` and reloads the list.
  - Pages hold 50 records; Load more records appends them.
  - Empty, 401/403 sign-in, 503/5xx/network-unavailable (content kept and dimmed) and refresh (dimmed, `aria-busy`) states are covered.
  - The panel binds to every `[data-tab="rag-administration"]` link, and its init is idempotent; open disclosures stay open across refreshes.
- **App connector card.** The sync result line now comes from `syncCountParts(stats)`, which lists processed, deleted, skipped and **withdrawn**, each only when it is above 0. The rendered output for processed, deleted and skipped is unchanged.
- Chat, strict mode, webSearch with Exa, `source_images`, `{{context}}`, voice and embed are untouched. There is no new env variable, switch, SQL or migration.
