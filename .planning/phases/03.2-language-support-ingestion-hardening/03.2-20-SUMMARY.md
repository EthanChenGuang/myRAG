# Phase 3.2 Plan 20: Language-aware sentence splitting in the TS chunkers Summary

**With `RAG_LANGUAGE_AWARE_INGEST=on`, the TS chunkers split each over-long block at the sentence ends of its own language: 。！？ for zh and .!? for en and it, never at a semicolon. Pieces are re-joined with nothing for zh and with one space for en and it. With the switch off, every chunker output on the ingest fixtures is byte-identical to a snapshot taken before the change.**

## Behaviour

- **Off (the default):** every chunker receives `legacySplitter`, which is today's two expressions and joiner, and the language settings are never read. `chunk-legacy-snapshot.json` holds sha256 digests for all ingest fixtures (`eval/fixtures/ingest/text/*.md` and the recorded parse Markdown). They cover three chunker kinds at three budgets plus the three parent-child modes, and they are unchanged.
- **On:** the chunk stage, the profile write stage (`embed-write`) and `rag:rechunk` all pass the switch, so profiles are rebuilt with the same rule. Token counting is still `countTokens`, which uses cl100k_base.
- **Docling path:** chunks that come from Docling are still returned as they are. Plan 03.2-23 splits them in the sidecar.
- **Table rows** are never split. Only text blocks get the language rule.
- **No new switch, env variable, migration or store.** No purge hook or store-inventory registration was needed.
