# Phase 03.2 Plan 50: queued translate jobs

**With asynchronous ingestion, translation after publish is a ledger job of kind `translate`: the post-publish hook enqueues it, the sidecar worker claims it and only calls back, and the app translates it in time-boxed slices through `POST /api/internal/rag/ingest/jobs/[id]/translate`.**
