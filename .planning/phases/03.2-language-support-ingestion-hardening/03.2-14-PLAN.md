Decide upload approval at `identify` and hold at `publish` in the unified pipeline, inline and queued (D-36, D-37, D-56), and add the post-publish seam later plans hook into.

Purpose: a member's upload is processed completely but stays out of retrieval and the cache until an admin approves it.
Output: decision module, stage changes, `pending_review` in the TypeScript and ledger status sets, the `afterPublish` seam, unit and end-to-end tests.

Deploy order: the sidecar first (it must know the ledger status `pending_review`), then the app.
</objective>
