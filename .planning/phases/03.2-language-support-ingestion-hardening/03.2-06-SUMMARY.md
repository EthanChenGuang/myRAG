# Phase 3.2 Plan 06: Query-side, model-output and golden contracts Summary

**Nine new Pydantic contracts with generated Zod, plus the scope filter, the translation leg weight and the golden table_split additions. Nothing that serialized before changes a byte.**

## Accomplishments

- `scopes.py`: `ScopeFilter`, `ScopeVocabularyEntry` (dimension, slug value, label, unique aliases, active), `ScopeTagSuggestion` (unique dimension/value pairs, source `rule | model`, at most 30).
- `RetrievalFilters` gains `scopes` and `include_untagged`. `RetrievalLegWeights` gains `translation` (> 0). The committed `eval/config/retrieval-params.json` round-trips exactly and its `config_hash` is unchanged. `params-hash.test.ts` passes.
- `versions.py`, `translation.py`, `embed.py` with the models listed above. All are frozen with `extra="forbid"`.
- Golden: `table_split` category, `TableAnswer`, `NativeReview`. All 210 committed golden lines (164 + 46, more than the 192 the plan counted) validate. Their dumps keep the pre-plan key set, nested filter keys included. The parity test was committed before the change and passed on the old code.
- Samples: one for each new model, plus variants `RetrievalFilters:scopes`, `RetrievalParams:translation_weight`, `SupersessionCandidate:confirmed`, `TranslationSettings:unconfigured` and `GoldenItem:table_split`. All sample content is neutral.

## Deviations from Plan

- The golden parity test compares the dumped key sets (item and nested `retrieval_filters`) and the re-validated model. It does not compare raw line equality, because the committed lines are partial dumps: they omit default nested keys.
- Additional rules: `include_untagged` without `scopes` is rejected, and `table_answer` on a category other than `table_split` is rejected, in Pydantic only. They keep the contract unambiguous for plans 30 and 42.
