Build the one publish-hold mechanism in SQL (research F-1, Pattern 4): the publish function refuses a pending or held document, and approval, rejection and hold release go through service-role functions.

Purpose: the hold is enforced by the database, so no ingestion path, connector or direct RPC call can publish an unapproved upload (SEC-09), and held `reparse` batches reuse the same guard (LANG-04).
Output: migration 0803 and its tests.
