## Rules that apply to every plan

- **Owner rule of 2026-11-25 (illustrations are not product content).** The sample subjects, questions and numbers that CONTEXT, RESEARCH, UI-SPEC and ROADMAP use to illustrate capabilities are examples only. No plan asks for them, and nothing a plan makes the executor build may contain them: no product code, config, lexicon, prompt, rule or default with a subject-specific word or value. Fixtures and eval items are specified by structure (for example "a long table whose answer row lies beyond the first chunk, with gold evidence on the row and its header"), their content is synthetic, neutral and varied in subject, chosen by the executor and named in the SUMMARY, and acceptance criteria assert behaviour on those shapes, never specific strings or values. Where a locked decision quotes an example (D-21 serialized row, D-45 sample question, D-18 sample words, the "Specific Ideas" list), the plans implement the capability and treat the quoted text as an illustration. Recorded as a prohibition in plan 26 and applied in plans 13, 25 to 32, 41 to 47, 60, 64 and 68.
- **Switches.** Every new switch defaults to off or to today's behaviour (D-47). Plan 03 lands all eighteen variables at once; later plans only read `ctx.config` or the RAG config. Plan 66 proves all-off parity.
- **Tracer first.** Each plan leads with one `type="tracer"` task that carries one path end to end with a runnable check; plan 36 leads with its blocking package checkpoint and the tracer follows it.
- **Verification commands.** Every `<automated>` command is followed by `<fails_when>`. Count gates carry a baseline with the commit it was measured at.
- **Design summary gate.** The phase has a UI-SPEC, so `scripts/ci/check-frontend-design-summary.mjs` checks every SUMMARY: every plan's `<output>` requires the `## Design skills` table. UI plans (03, 11, 56 to 62, 64, and 67 when it changes `index.html`) record real outcomes of frontend-design, Impeccable and web-design-guidelines; the others record `n/a`, `n/a`, `none`.
- **Threat ids.** `T-03.2-<plan number><digit>` (for example `T-03.2-141`), unique within the phase; `T-03.2-SC` is in every plan.
- **Trunk moves.** Other phases are executing. Each migration plan re-checks `supabase/migrations/` and starts from the newest definition of every function it replaces, located by name. Contract plans rebase and regenerate, never hand-merge generated files. Line numbers that remain in `read_first` entries for TypeScript, Python, HTML or workflow files are orientation hints from planning time; the symbol or passage named beside them is what to find.
