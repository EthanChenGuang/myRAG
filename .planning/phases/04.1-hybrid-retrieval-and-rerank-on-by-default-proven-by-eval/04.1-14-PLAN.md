- `applyRefusal` in `src/lib/rag/v2/stages/rank.ts` passes `floors` derived from `evalFloorCalibration(profile)` (the prechunked fixture calibration in eval) or the embedding profile's calibration to `legacyRuleFloor`, while `filterCandidates` (`src/lib/rag/context/assemble.ts`) calls `legacyKeepReason` with the legacy keep floors.
