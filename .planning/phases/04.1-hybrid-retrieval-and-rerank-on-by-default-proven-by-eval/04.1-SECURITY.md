8. Out of scope, flag for the 04.3 audit: `dispatchRetrieval` passes the `RAG_DOC_ACCESS_GROUPS` filter only to `runV2Pipeline`; legacy `searchByEmbedding` sends no `p_access_keys` (04.3 default off; tenant scoping unaffected).
