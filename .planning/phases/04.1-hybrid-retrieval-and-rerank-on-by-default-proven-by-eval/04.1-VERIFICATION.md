# Phase 04.1: Hybrid retrieval and rerank on by default, proven by eval — Verification Report

**Phase goal:** An install that has chosen its supported languages and sets nothing else gets hybrid retrieval and reranking, refuses no-answer questions at least as reliably as today without refusing more answerable ones, and can return to today's behaviour exactly with `RAG_PIPELINE=legacy`, with all of this shown on the eval before the defaults change; until the languages are chosen, or while the database lacks the Phase 4 functions, the install keeps serving the legacy pipeline.

**Verdict: `human_needed`**

- **(a) The literal goal was not delivered.** The defaults were not flipped.
  - The pre-registered gate failed twice: in 04.1-07, and again in the 04.1-17 re-run after gap closure.
  - `eval/decisions/04.1-retrieval-defaults.json` has `status: not_flipped` and `provisional: true`.
  - The owner closed 04.1-09 as "not flipped" on 2026-11-26.
  - What ships: `src/lib/rag/config.ts` `DEFAULT_PIPELINE = 'legacy'`; `src/lib/env.ts` falls back to legacy / false / none / false / similarity; the eval pin is `RAG_PIPELINE=legacy`.
- **(b) The evidence machinery was delivered and did its job.** Nothing was flipped without passing proof. That machinery is the gate tool, the pre-registered criteria with sha256 provenance, 11+4 run configs, the decision builder and `check-current`, the committed gate report, G8 negative controls, the CI legacy tiers and the single resolver. Thresholds and baselines were not edited to make the gate pass. The phase's core rule ("defaults change only with eval evidence") holds.
- **Why `human_needed` and not PASS_WITH_GAPS or FAIL:**
  - Criterion 1, criterion 5, DFLT-01 and DFLT-07 are not met. They can only be closed by an owner override that accepts the not-flipped closure, plus the operator's live passes.
  - **With the override recorded** (text below) and gaps G-01 to G-06 handled, this becomes **PASS_WITH_GAPS**.
  - **Without the override**, judged against the literal goal, it is **FAIL**.
