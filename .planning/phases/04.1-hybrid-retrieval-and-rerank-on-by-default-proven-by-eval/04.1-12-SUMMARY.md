# Phase 4.1 Plan 12: What an operator who sets nothing gets (not-flipped path)

**With nothing set, every entry point (chat, embed, voice, eval) in both tenancy modes is served by legacy. It equals explicit `RAG_PIPELINE=legacy` byte for byte on the compared fields, never calls `hybrid_search_chunks`, reads no language settings and logs no startup line. It also works on a database without the Phase 4 functions, with no fallback and no `[RAG Config]` error. An explicit `RAG_PIPELINE=v2` fails loudly: `backend_error` without `hybrid_search_chunks`, `chunking_profiles_unavailable` on ingest without `rag_list_chunking_profiles`, and `languages_not_configured` on every profile without languages. The CI-equivalent job list passes with the shipped defaults, and both legacy tiers are all-zero.**
