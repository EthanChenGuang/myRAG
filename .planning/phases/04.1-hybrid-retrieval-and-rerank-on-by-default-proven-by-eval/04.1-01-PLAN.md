<objective>
Give v2 a fallback refusal floor with today's legacy refusal semantics, behind a new switch whose default keeps v2 exactly as it is.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-03 and research F-1. Under v2 the similarity floor refuses about 112 of 114 fixture items, and in production the rerank gate is not applied for any request until live cells exist (gate.ts:100), so the floor decides what users see. Legacy keeps a candidate when a query word appears in its document; v2 has no such rule. This plan ports that rule into the floor through one shared predicate.
Output: `relevance.ts` with the shared predicate, `legacyRuleFloor`, the `RAG_REFUSAL_FLOOR` parse, and tests.

Runs after Phase 4's automated close (04-42). Config-location sync of the new variable is plan 04.1-02 (same wave, disjoint files); the default stays `similarity` until plan 04.1-09.
