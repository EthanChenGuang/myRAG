# Phase 05.1.1 Plan 21: Ship check and the weekly-report job Summary

**"Prove it on the domain test set before going live" is now a command with a pass or fail answer, and the weekly report runs on a schedule only where private data exists.**

## Accomplishments

- `python -m rag_eval.ship_check --baseline <report.json> --candidate <report.json> --target <metric>` refuses non-real populations (`not_real_population`), different manifests (`manifest_mismatch`), different gated item sets (`item_set_mismatch`) and sets without real items (`no_gated_items`). It then fails on any slice that drops beyond the noise band (`slice_regression_<slice>`) and on a target whose seeded paired bootstrap 95% interval does not clear zero (`target_not_improved`). The `ShipCheckResult` goes to `eval/golden/private/ship/` in every case. A failed check exits 1 and lists its reasons.
- `nightly.yml` has a `weekly-report` job (Monday `23 4 * * 1` or manual). Its first step sets `HAS_PRIVATE_EVAL` from `eval/golden/private` and prints a `::notice::` when the data is absent. Every later step is gated on it. The job runs `failure_report` and then `weekly_report`. It uploads no artifact, and the secret reaches it only through step env.
- `check-workflows.py` enforces all of that.

## Deviations

- `eval/src/rag_eval/paired.py` is not in `files_modified`. `_bootstrap_interval` gained `seed` and `resamples` keyword arguments, defaulting to the old constants, so that the ship check reuses it. Callers see no change, and the full eval suite passes.
- The plan's reason `slice_regression:<lang>` cannot be stored, because the `ReasonCode` pattern forbids `:`. It is stored as `slice_regression_<slice>` and printed as `slice_regression:<slice>:<metric>`.
- The four existing nightly jobs gained a job-level `if` that excludes the weekly cron.
- Additional rules beyond the five in the plan: the job must keep the weekly-only trigger, checkout must use `clean: false`, and both report modules plus a locked eval sync must be present.
