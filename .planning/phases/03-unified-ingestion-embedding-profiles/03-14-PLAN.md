Decisions inside the plan:
- The optional LLM fill-in behind RAG_INVOICE_LLM_EXTRACT (D-26) runs in the app, not here, because the app already holds the model credentials and the five-location model list; plan 03-29 implements it with InvoiceFields as the structured-output schema and a recorded fake. - Synthetic ids follow research: Partita IVA 99999999990, USCC 91000000MA0000000N, invoice numbers with a letter prefix and four-digit year such as FT-2025-00417, chosen to avoid the fixture PII scanner's phone shapes (pitfall P-21).
