<objective>
Scan ingested documents for prompt-injection phrasing in zh, en and it with rules stored as data, and quarantine matches so the no-switch D-08 filter keeps them out of every retrieval (D-30, D-31, SEC-01).

Purpose: success criterion 7: an injection fixture is quarantined at ingestion and never appears in any retrieval result until an admin releases it (release and delete come in plan 03-37).
Output: the rules file, the reusable matcher (Phase 5.1's SEC-03 imports it), the scan stage, and tests across all five retrieval profiles.

Runs after Phase 2 is complete (every 02-* plan has a SUMMARY).

Deviations and limits (from research):
- Research Pattern 11 adds zero-width/bidi stripping, whitespace-free matching for Chinese (OCR spacing) and ReDoS limits to D-30's NFKC case-folded matching.
- The scan acts only under RAG_INGEST_PIPELINE=unified; documents written by the legacy writers are not scanned. This is a documented limit of the legacy default; the D-08 filter itself applies to both pipelines.
- The matcher module lives at src/lib/rag/security/ (not under ingest/) so Phase 5.1 can reuse it at retrieval time without ingestion types.
