<objective>
Create the typed ingestion fixtures (D-23, D-42): synthetic specs, a deterministic PDF generator, recorded ParseResult and Docling JSON from the real adapter, a manifest the fake sidecar serves by file hash, and the optional nightly job that runs real Docling on the same PDFs.

Purpose: success criteria 1, 4, 6, 7 and 9 are shown in ingest mode on these fixtures, without live services and without committing binaries of unknown origin.
Output: specs, generator, recorder, committed PDFs and recordings, manifest, tests, PII scanner update and the nightly job.

Runs after Phase 2 is complete (every 02-* plan has a SUMMARY).

Decisions inside the plan:
- Digital PDFs use reportlab with `invariant=1` and the built-in STSong-Light CID font for Chinese, so they are byte-reproducible without font files; scanned PDFs are rendered to images with Pillow and a CJK font passed by `--cjk-font`, the font's sha256 is recorded in the manifest, and their byte check runs only when that font is present (skipped with a notice otherwise).
- Recorded outputs are built with docling-core from the specs, not by running full Docling here (not installable in this environment); the nightly job and the ROADMAP live verification compare real conversions with them.
- Synthetic ids follow pitfall P-21 (Partita IVA 99999999990, USCC 91000000MA0000000N, invoice numbers like FT-2025-00417).
