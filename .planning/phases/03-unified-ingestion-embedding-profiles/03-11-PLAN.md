Decisions inside the plan:
- Research "parallel-document trap": gold facts come only from single-language documents (zh: employee-handbook, scanned-contract; en: shipping-handbook, warehouse-floorplan, scanned-invoice; it: warranty-terms, scanned-receipt). One fact may be asked from both other languages (for example the same zh fact asked in en and in it), so four facts per document language serve both pairs and D-41's "add documents only if needed" is met without adding corpus documents. Not changing the corpus is what keeps paired differences on old items at zero.
- Each pair mixes two items that share a digit or Latin identifier with the evidence and two purely semantic items, so the slice under the fixture embedder (not multilingual) is not uniformly zero (research §"Evaluation Additions").
