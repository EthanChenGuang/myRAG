### 4. 0983 rag_scope_centroids_rebuild
- The rebuild returns only `(group_kind, groups, chunks)` counts and is service_role only (`0983:54-58,498,514`). Centroid vectors and group keys are read by `rag_scope_centroids_list` / `rag_scope_status`, also service_role only. Callers are `scope/centroids.ts` (scoring) and `scope/admin.ts` (admin status, where admins are `admin_all` anyway).
- Centroids **do** average restricted chunk embeddings. The only output reaching a non-admin is the gate decision: `ScopeGateResult{label, score, negative_score, centroid_count, reason}` (contracts.generated.ts:1066), with no group key. - **EXEMPT is acceptable (low).** The sentinel lists it (`tests/rag-db/access-catalog-sentinel.test.mts:206-209`, commit 87438357) and passes. Follow-up: keep `scope_gate.score` out of any client payload, and consider per-access-set centroids if the gate ever returns group keys.

### 6. Share route
