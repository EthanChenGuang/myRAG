## Findings that change later plans (plan 02)

1. **Most seams the research listed as "planned" are on trunk now.** Phase 3.2 (translations, scopes, `checkAdminOrOrgAdmin`, UI hosts), Phase 4.2 (table rows, duplicate clusters, identifiers, versions) and Phase 5.1.1 (retrieval log, review queue) have landed. Plans 03, 10, 11 and 13 extend the existing objects instead of using the D-30 absent-seam guards.
2. **`hybrid_search_chunks` newest definition is `0856_hybrid_search_42.sql` (26 parameters).** It adds `p_version_scope jsonb` and `p_identifiers_norm text[]` after 0802's `p_scopes`, `p_scopes_include_untagged`, `p_effective_on`. 0930 must drop every older signature (20 parameters in 0407/0413, 21 in 0602, 24 in 0802, 26 in 0856) and create the new one on top of the 0856 body.
3. **`rag_exact_id_documents` has two signatures on trunk**: `(text, text[], integer)` from 0314/0425 and `(text, text[], integer, jsonb, text[])` from 0856. 0930 drops both.
4. **Phase 4.2 adds content-returning functions that no plan of this phase names**: `rag_table_lookup` (0861), `rag_identifier_resolve` and `rag_identifier_candidates` (0858), `rag_dup_cluster_sources` (0862). Per the planning note in the executor brief they must take `p_access_keys` / `p_access_all` and be classified in `tests/rag-db/access-catalog-sentinel.test.mts`. Plan 11 owns this; `rag_table_rows` also needs an `access_groups` column (0852's header leaves it open for 4.3), which plan 03/04's 0921 should add. **Done by plan 11 in 0933** (see the table above).
5. **`public.rag_security_events` already exists (Phase 5.2, `0942_rag_security_events.sql`)** with a different shape (source, action, detector, rule ids, one document and chunk id) and alert counting in 0943. The D-26 store of this phase is therefore **`public.rag_access_security_events`** (0920). Plan 05's `rag_security_event_record`, plan 07's `recordSecurityEvent`, plan 14 and plan 53's catalog query use that name.
6. **Phase 7 objects are partly on trunk**: `rag_validate_chunks(text, uuid[], text)` (0704) and `src/lib/rag/backend/external.ts` / `revalidate.ts`. `rag_validate_chunks_for_groups`, a Qdrant or Weaviate adapter module beyond `qdrant/scoped-request.ts`, a canary factory and a `backend-canary` CI job are absent. Under A-14 Phase 7 owns them; recorded only.
7. **Block 0920 to 0939 was free on every origin branch** before 0920 was committed. The 09xx files on trunk are 0940 to 0944 (Phase 5.2) and 0960 to 0978 (Phase 5.3 / 6.2).
