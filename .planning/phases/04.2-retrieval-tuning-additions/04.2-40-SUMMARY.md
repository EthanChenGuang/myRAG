## What 0936 does
- **The predicate.** It is identical in all five sites (two in `rag_exact_id_documents`) after placeholder folding, and sits between `/* identifier-document-filter:begin */` and `:end`:
  `c.document_id IN (SELECT ic.document_id FROM public.document_chunks ic WHERE ic.tenant_key = <tenant> AND ic.status IN ('ready','variant') AND ic.identifiers_norm && <ids> AND (<anchor keys> IS NULL OR ic.access_groups && <anchor keys>))`.
  - `<tenant>` is `$2` / `p_tenant_key`, the key `rag_assert_tenant_key` checked.
  - The subquery is uncorrelated.
- **Bind positions.** No new bind position or parameter. In the dynamic functions the access slot (`$24` in hybrid, `$31` in bm25) carries `v_anchor_keys`. That equals `p_access_keys` on a restricted call, and the 0930/0932 access predicate is appended only then. On any other call the slot is NULL.
- **Unchanged behaviour:** the NULL-identifier branch, the 100-identifier cap, the `access_scope_required` and `access_keys_empty` guards, and the predicates on the searched chunk and its document.
- **Catalog:** SECURITY INVOKER throughout, `SET search_path = ''`, and one overload of each function. Revoke from PUBLIC, anon and authenticated; grant to service_role.
- **Precheck (55000):** the four functions must have the identifier and access parameters, `document_chunks` must have `identifiers_norm` and `access_groups`, and the bm25 argument list must be exactly the 0932 one.
