# 04.2-38 SUMMARY: reserved Phase 4.2 purge hooks (migration 0864)

## What changed
- `supabase/migrations/0864_purge_hooks.sql` (0864 was free; no renumbering):
  - `rag_purge_hook_table_rows(p_tenant_key text, p_lineage_id uuid, p_document_ids uuid[]) RETURNS bigint` redefines the 0852 body. It is now SECURITY INVOKER (was SECURITY DEFINER) and deletes only the listed versions' `rag_table_rows`. Before, it also deleted the whole lineage. It returns the number of rows removed.
  - `rag_purge_hook_duplicate_clusters(...)` (same arguments) does five things for the listed versions of the tenant:
    - clears `dup_cluster_id` and `simhash` on their chunks;
    - deletes their chunks' `rag_dup_chunk_bands` rows;
    - removes their ids from `rag_dup_clusters.source_document_ids` and marks those clusters dirty;
    - recomputes the touched clusters through `rag_dup_clusters_recompute`, in batches of 1000, so a one-member cluster dissolves (D-20);
    - returns the number of chunk memberships removed.
  - Both hooks call `rag_assert_tenant_key` first. They raise 22023 and change nothing when a listed id belongs to another tenant, or to another lineage when `p_lineage_id` is given. Every statement filters on the tenant. Both use `SET search_path = ''`, are revoked from PUBLIC, anon and authenticated, and are granted to service_role only.
- 0410: added a guarded service_role-only entry for `rag_purge_hook_duplicate_clusters`. The entry for `rag_purge_hook_table_rows` already existed.
- `tests/rag-db/phase42-schema.test.mts`: the re-apply idempotency test now re-runs 0864 after 0851-0854, matching the AGENTS.md rule to re-run later redefinitions in order. Without that, re-applying 0852 restores the old DEFINER body.
- RET-15: no hook is needed. The identifier columns go with their chunk and document rows. `rag_identifier_patterns` holds tenant settings and is exempt.
