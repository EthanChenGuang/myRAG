# 04.2-11 hand-off: where voice TTFT is measured (D-38 i, D-39)

For the coordinator.

- The voice answer is streamed by the voice agent's own LLM client, not by the app. `/api/internal/voice-chat` is not on the voice answer path, so voice time to first token cannot be measured there.
- The measurement point is the voice agent: `VoicePipeline.processUserMessage` in `voice-agent/src/voice_agent.ts`.
  - `pre_generation_ms`: from the start of `processUserMessage` to the moment the agent starts its LLM call.
  - `ttft_ms`: from the start of `processUserMessage` to the first non-empty text delta of that LLM stream.
- LAT-04's "server-side" wording is delivered for voice as "first text delta of the agent's LLM stream, posted to the app": the agent posts `{ traceId, ttft_ms, pre_generation_ms }` to `/api/internal/voice-timing`, which writes two NUMERIC Langfuse scores (`ttft_ms`, `pre_generation_ms`) on the retrieval trace and one log line with ids and numbers only.
- First audio frame latency is not measured (deferred).
- The CI pre-generation gate for voice covers the `voice-rag` handler time (D-39).
