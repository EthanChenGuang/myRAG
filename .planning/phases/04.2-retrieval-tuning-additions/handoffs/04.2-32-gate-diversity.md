## Cause (diagnosed, not fixed: a failed gate is a result, not a reason to tune)
- The Phase 5.2 fixture loads `p52-rules-v1.*.md` and `p52-rules-v2.*.md` as two separate **current** documents (no lineage). The trace has no `is_current` or `document_version` on the kept chunks.
- Matching sections of v1 and v2 differ only in one value. Example from the `en-stale-version-003` trace: "spectators stand at least 15 metres" (v1) and "... 20 metres" (v2). The `diversify` stage puts them in one near-duplicate cluster (`collapsed: 20` of 75) and keeps the higher-scored representative. Here that is the v1 chunk, so the v2 gold chunk drops out of the top 10.
- Product effect: with `RAG_RESULT_DIVERSITY=collapse|mmr`, two documents that are near-copies but carry conflicting values can lose the correct one. That is the conflict case Phase 5.2 tests. Off (the shipped default) is unaffected.

## Options for the owner or coordinator (no option chosen here)
1. Diversity never collapses chunks of different documents when their texts differ in a number or another value token. The cluster key or the collapse rule would compare value tokens, not only the SimHash band.
2. Collapse only inside one document lineage or one source, never across distinct current documents.
3. Model the 5.2 fixture versions as one lineage (v1 superseded by v2) so the version scope removes v1 before diversify. This changes the fixture, not the product; it does not cover tenants that upload two current near-copies.
4. Exclude `stale_version` from the diversity slice check. That loosens the spec and needs an owner decision; the plan forbids it here.
