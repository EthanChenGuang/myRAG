<objective>
Complete the Pydantic contract set for Phase 1 and prove the Pydantic-to-Zod pipeline in both directions: every structure the eval runner, baseline, gate, judge, drafting tool and sidecar exchange is defined once as a Pydantic v2 model (per D-06), exported to JSON Schema, generated into zod/v3, and checked by tests that fail when the generated files are stale.

Purpose: EVAL-02 and EVAL-03 — one source of truth for every contract, with golden-set rules (D-04, never chunk IDs) enforced in the model itself. Interface-first: plans 01-05, 01-06, 01-09, 01-10 and 01-11 consume these models and must not add contracts of their own.
Output: five new contract modules, a samples generator, regenerated schemas and Zod file, and four test files.

Decision labels D-01..D-25 are defined in 01-01-PLAN.md's objective (planner-assigned labels for 01-CONTEXT.md decisions in document order).
</objective>
