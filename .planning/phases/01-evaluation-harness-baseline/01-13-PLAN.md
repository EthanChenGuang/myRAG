  <behavior>
    - an answerable item with 2 spans, one hit at kept rank 5 and one at rank 6: recall@5 0.5, recall@10 1.0, MRR 0.2
    - gold chunk at rank exactly 5 counts for recall@5; at rank 6 it does not; an item with 3 kept chunks and its gold at rank 3 has recall@10 1.0
    - swapping the order of an item's spans gives identical ItemResult values
    - a refused answerable item scores recall 0 and MRR 0 and counts toward false-refusal; a refused no_answer item counts toward correct-refusal; an unrefused no_answer item does neither
    - a language slice with zero unanswerable items reports correct_refusal_rate value None with n 0
    - Chinese, Italian and full-width-digit chunks are scored as hits
    - aggregate keeps unrounded floats (for example 1/3 stays 0.333333...)
  </behavior>
  <action>
Per D-07 (no LLM judge) and D-13: `score_items(traces, golden)` returns one ItemResult per golden item: rank is the 1-based position in the trace's `kept` list (Filter 1 survivors in final order, what reaches the context); spans_hit_at_k counts spans matched by any of the top-k kept chunks (each span once); first_hit_rank is the first kept rank matching any span; refused comes from the trace. `aggregate(results, k_values=(5, 10))` builds SliceMetrics for overall, by_language, by_category and by_language_category (key `<language>/<category>`): recall_at_k = mean over answerable items of spans_hit_at_k / span_count; mrr = mean over answerable items of 1/first_hit_rank (0 when none); correct_refusal_rate = refused unanswerable / unanswerable; false_refusal_rate = refused answerable / answerable; each a MetricValue with its n and value None when n is 0. Keep every value at full float precision in this module.
  </action>
