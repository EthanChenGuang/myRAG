# Phase 3.1.1 Plan 11: Domain fixture pack Summary

**A synthetic zh/en/it pack for contract_legal and financial_report: six documents, 45 golden items and a frozen dev/test split, kept apart from the main corpus so no baseline moves.**

## Accomplishments
- Six documents in the main corpus format: a fixture-meta doc_type line, `##` sections and `<!-- chunk -->` markers.
  - Each contract has 7–8 numbered sections, 5 defined terms that later sections use again, at least 2 cross-references and one section that makes an exception to another.
  - Each financial report has a table of 5 rows plus a total row and 3–4 numeric columns. Every column total equals the sum of its rows, figures use the language's number format, and two narrative sections quote table figures.
- 45 items: 7 per cell for contract_legal and 8 per cell for financial_report. Each cell has exact_term, general, compound, colloquial, table (financial only), one no_answer item and two cross_lingual items. Every quote occurs verbatim in exactly one section of its document. The split is dev 23 / test 22 (contract_legal 11/10, financial_report 12/12).
- The test also checks the PII rules: it reuses the phone and email checker from `test_fixture_pii.py` and adds tax-ID and IBAN patterns, with a self-test for each.

## Task Commits
1. **Task 1: fixture test and the six documents.** RED `a6cc750` (test), GREEN `482f232` (feat). The GREEN commit also corrects the test's REQUIREMENTS.md parsing: it now takes the parenthesis that follows "Examples", not the first parenthesis in the paragraph.

## Domain neutrality
- en contract_legal: a licence for river-gauge water-level data between two invented hydrometric and analytics companies.
- it contract_legal: a rental contract for stage-lighting equipment between an invented stage-technology firm and a theatre company.
- zh contract_legal: a joint research agreement on a matte glaze formulation between two invented ceramics companies.
- en financial_report: the annual report of an invented community solar cooperative (generation and revenue by site).
- it financial_report: the half-year report of an invented publisher (revenue and margin by product line).
- zh financial_report: the annual report summary of an invented aquaculture company (output, revenue and gross profit by product).

The pack reuses no illustration from the Domain neutrality paragraph of `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`, and none of the extra brief terms (lodging, VPN, password, branch). The test reads that list and fails on any match in en documents or any item text. It also fails if a party name or capitalised multi-word name from the main corpus appears in the pack, or if two pack documents share a party name.

## Deviations from Plan
- **Category "paraphrase" does not exist** in the GoldenItem contract. Paraphrased questions use `general`. Financial cells also have a `table` item.
- **Split balancing.** The plan asks for at least 40% of each doc type in each split. The repository's hash rule targets 70/30, so item numbers were chosen to balance the split under that rule, which leaves gaps in the numbering. The rule string and `assign_split` stay unchanged.
- **Doc type on items.** Every item has a `doc_type:<type>` tag, because no-answer items have no evidence document to take a doc type from. Plan 12 can filter items on this tag.
