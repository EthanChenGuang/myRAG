# Quick 261128-d28: D-28 revision and plan 04.1-18 rewrite — Summary

Plan 04.1-18 is rewritten around the cause its first execution measured, and CONTEXT D-28 carries a dated revision. Only files under `.planning/` changed. No product code, eval config, gate report, decision record or default was touched.

## Why

The first execution of 04.1-18 stopped at Task 1 (`04.1-18-SUMMARY.md`, status `stopped`). In ingest mode v2-default refuses 21 answerable items that legacy answers, 16 of them on the gated dev split. 19 of the 21 (all 16 gated) are items legacy answers through `exact_id` rows at synthetic similarity 1.0, which D-28 kept out of the `legacy_rule` pool. The 200-character preview, which the plan had assumed to be the cause, accounts for 1 item and none on the gated split.

## Planner decision: the two findings of the stop report

Both are fixed inside 04.1-18, in Task 2b, before the gate run.

- The committed prechunked floor was fitted on an older golden set (`golden_sha256` mismatch).
- The harness applies a floor without checking its hash.

The harness will stop a run on a hash mismatch, with a named error, and both entries (prechunked and ingest) are refitted by the tool with the committed rule and target. I chose a loud stop over a silent fall back to 0.65, because the fall back would change what the `similarity` candidate runs on without a trace. Consequence the owner should know: the prechunked floor value will probably change, so `v2-default-similarity-floor` in prechunked mode runs on a different floor than in 04.1-17. The plan requires the SUMMARY to report the old and new value as a finding of its own.
