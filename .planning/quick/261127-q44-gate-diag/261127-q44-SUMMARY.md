## G1: diagnostic (option B, report only)

The command was `uv run --directory eval python -m rag_eval.phase44_dup_diag --out eval/reports/phase-04.4/diagnostics`: 12 harness runs, 3 groups × 2 arms × 2 modes. The conclusion is in `eval/reports/phase-04.4/diagnostics/README.md`.

**Verdict: BM25 itself (IDF), not ties and not missing de-duplication.**

- **The lost span is the flood passage, not the solo document.** `dup-solo` stays at rank 1 in both arms. The flood passage is copied into 30 documents, so its terms get a low BM25 IDF, and bm25 ranks unrelated generic chunks above its copies. `ts_rank_cd` has no IDF.
- **Ties do not explain the difference.** They are equally common in both arms: 9 of 9 items have a tie, and in 9 of 9 the tie crosses rank 5, in both modes. `ts_rank_cd` ties slightly more hits.
- **De-duplication does not help.** `RAG_DUP_CLUSTERS=true`, alone or with `RAG_RESULT_DIVERSITY=collapse`, leaves the prechunked drop unchanged. Clusters are built and collapse is active (`also_in` on 36 traces), but it folds copies after the lexical ranking, and the document frequencies stay the same.
- **Limitation:** the ingest fixture builds no duplicate clusters, so the ingest de-duplication arms equal the baseline by construction.
- **`templated_collection`:** no drop beyond the band. bm25 has more near-duplicate ties there (24 against 15 items), which is the first thing to check if it moves again.
- **Proposed lever, not built:** cluster-aware document frequency for BM25. That would be a scoring design change for a later plan.

"Real-set near-duplicate coverage" is in `04.4-15-PLAN.md` Task 3: how-to-verify, the automated verify and acceptance criteria.
